# Set 17 · 217–226 · Videotex as its makers dreamed it: entertainment, games, arts and messaging

The ten concepts of group E of the videotex research (concepts 41 to 50): what the period promised a
terminal in the living room would be for once the news and the bank had been read. Every service,
brand, place and person is invented; the medium is never drawn as a logo.

| Page | Title | Envisioned use | Plays to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 217 | Chess by Wire | Chess against another subscriber in another town, one move a night, from the earliest promotion. | DRCS pieces, rectangle rows, BLINK on the last move and cursor. |
| 218 | The Maze | A first-person dungeon adventure walked one keypress at a time, the most popular game of late services. | Graded perspective polygons, a mosaic mini-map, a BLINK torch, WAIT by depth. |
| 219 | Quiz Night | The evening quiz, played against the clock; quizzes were among the most-read pages everywhere. | A WAIT countdown, a colour-map reveal that dims the wrong answers, BLINK. |

## Decisions taken on the concepts

- **217:** the research's "PLAYER 2 (OTTAWA)" names a real city; the two households are in the invented towns of Pine Hollow and Ashford Bay, and the telephone wire between them is drawn on the score panel.
- **218:** the service is *Questline*, the dungeon the Vaults of Merrow; the research's "TURN LEFT 4 · RIGHT 6" becomes a three-way keypad prompt with "8 AHEAD", and a mosaic map is added because mosaics are the medium's other picture language.
- **219:** the answer is revealed by the colour map alone: the three wrong answers' entries are redefined to grey, so nothing is redrawn.
- **220:** the artist is the invented M. Ostrander; the dusk is three colour-map steps from gold to violet, and the town's windows light on the last.
- **222:** the child is named Wren; the flower's petals are drawn while their entries are still the sky's colour, so that one SET COLOR opens it.
- **223:** the room is *Parlour*'s Night Owls room; no line of it quotes a real screen.
- **225:** the program is the invented game *Moonskip* from the invented *Homebyte* service; the concept's "page 812" is replaced by the frame count, since the page number is 225.
- **226:** a countdown (3, 2, 1) is added before the year, drawn while the sky is still empty.
- **Names:** service and product names were checked against names known to be in real use and coined where a first choice was a real product: the story service is *Bedtime*, the face kit *Phizkit*, the city service *Citylamp* and the game *Moonskip*.
