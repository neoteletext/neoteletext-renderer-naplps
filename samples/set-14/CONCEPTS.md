# Set 14 · Videotex as its makers dreamed it, group B · pages 187 to 196

Ten pictures of commerce, banking and shopping as Telidon, NAPLPS and the other videotex services were meant to deliver them: the electronic catalogue, the home bank, the advertisement that builds itself, the listing with its floor plan, the classified column, the live auction, the weekly grocery order, the box-office seat plan, the shopping-centre kiosk and the advertising band of a late page. Every service, shop, bank, place and person is invented. The concepts are group B of the research file (numbers 11 to 20), kept as written; the sharpening of each is noted here.

| Page | Title | Envisioned use | What was sharpened |
|---|---|---|---|
| 187 | The Electronic Catalogue | Shopping with a button press: a department store shows goods and takes the order. | Each panel draws in turn, price stars sit apart from the labels, and the order keys blink. |
| 188 | Your Balance, Mrs Okafor | Home banking: the customer sees balances and bills paid. | The vault door swings open in four steps before the statement prints; PAID stamps blink. |
| 189 | Build Your Credit | A bank advertisement built from a sequential drawing. | Coins stack step by step, the figure climbs by redrawn sky, the slogan comes last. |
| 190 | The Showhome on Cedar Crescent | Real-estate listing a buyer can read without leaving home. | The elevation, the plan, room labels and the mortgage line arrive in four stages. |
| 191 | Classifieds: Bicycles | Classified advertising as a screen revenue line. | A drawn ten-speed tops the column; one ad is framed in blinking cyan. |
| 192 | Going Once, Going Twice | Live auction, among Viewtron's favourite services. | The bid counter is seven-segment digits whose segments change by colour-map entries, three bids deep. |
| 193 | Grocery Order, Week of June 3 | Teleshopping groceries. | Items drop into a wire cart one by one; the cart's front wall is a macro redrawn over each; the total follows. |
| 194 | Theatre Tickets | Ordering seats from the sofa. | A pleated curtain opens on the stage, then the hall's seats appear row by row, built from a seat macro. |
| 195 | The Mall Directory | Kiosk pages, among NAPLPS's real successes. | Two floors, ten touch keys, a blinking YOU ARE HERE. |
| 196 | Today's Special at the Hardware Store | Advertising built into every page, the late business model. | The foot band rises to fill the page; a three-layer starburst chases through the colour map. |
