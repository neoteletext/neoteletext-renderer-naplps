# Round 4

Critique of the round-3 renders (`rounds/round-3/`), both sizes.

## Critique

**187.** With the mark in the band the page has an identity. It still ends the moment the third panel is drawn; the promise of the medium is that a key press does something, and nothing here answers one.

**188.** The door swing was four jumps, visible as steps. The statement is clean.

**189.** The confetti and skyline sell the payoff. The flag is a fixed shape; the sun sits behind where a flag unfurling would have to be repainted.

**190.** The plan reads, but nothing tells the viewer how to read it: it is a diagram, not a tour.

**191.** The ad with the highlighted frame carries the arrow glyphs that overlap its text at 640 and do not say what to do; a telephone is the clearer sign. The glints are too small to see at 480.

**192.** Clock hands and the pendulum now move with the bids. The plinth and the clock's top are bare; a spotlight would give the lot a stage.

**193.** Strong at 480. Little to fault; delivery line could show the van moving but would crowd the line.

**194.** The set is good but static. A scene that is meant to open a play should open on something happening.

**195.** The icons are blobs at 480, the book especially (a solid rectangle) and the envelope (a rectangle with a V cut in it but the cut was missing).

**196.** The two-stage page is the clearest story in the set. The old price is only grey text; an advertisement strikes it through.
