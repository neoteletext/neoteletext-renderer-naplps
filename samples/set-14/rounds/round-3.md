# Round 3

Critique of the round-2 renders (`rounds/round-2/`), both sizes.

## Critique

**187.** The settings make the three panels sit together. The service name is plain text on a red band: no mark, so no brand. The page ends in a static state.

**188.** The sunburst gives the opening real drama and the statement reads cleanly at 480. The statement lists what was paid but not what is owed, which is what home banking promised: reminders. Cramped bottom-right corner.

**189.** The skyline and shadows give the stair a place. After the slogan arrives the picture just stops; an advertisement that builds toward a payoff has no payoff beat.

**190.** The furnished plan reads; the lit windows are a quiet, good end. The elevation is static and the NEW listing is not announced.

**191.** Icons and coupon fill the page. The bike has no sparkle, and the chrome is flat.

**192.** The clock is the weakest object: its hands never move while a bidding war goes on. The ladder and counter are good.

**193.** Reads well now; the delivery line is plain text and the page says nothing about how the goods will come.

**194.** The fanned hall and the stage set are the best scene in the set. The marquee beads along the stage are static gold discs; there is nothing to say what the three price zones are.

**195.** Entrance gaps are missing, so the blocks look sealed; the icons are blobby at 480.

**196.** The article page paints in a single instant; the band's LOOK key does not draw the eye.
