## 176 · The Ember Tithe

A low viewpoint follows a river with a warm central reflection and cooler currents along the banks between tiered firs toward the citadel. Wind-sheared clouds and an asymmetric corona frame the eclipse. Wooded terraces and strips of low mist connect the riverbanks to the distant mountains, where sunset lights the snow faces above shaded gullies. Rock ledges step out from deep seams beneath the terrace and catch firelight below the gate. Light catches the dragon's wing ribs, folded foreleg, brow and throat plates. A twisting ribbon sheds embers and flares inside the receiving arch below a split-spire keep. A glowing flume leads from the receiving arch toward the coal suspended between the spires. Layered polygons shape the eclipse limb, and native filled arcs form the coal. Relative polygons shape the corona, stone, wings and trees, and point and line commands add sparks and ripples. The stream starts with a full RESET and defines all 16 colours. The scene is wordless and static. The eclipse uses a single horizontally stretched outline that shares the aspect distortion across both viewports.

Run `python3 set-12/validate.py` from `samples/` to check deterministic generation, stream framing, 7-bit encoding, the colour map, and both native-size renders. Add `--build` to regenerate the stream, manifest, PNGs and side-by-side contact sheet.
