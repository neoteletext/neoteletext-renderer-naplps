# Round 7 · The Ember Tithe

## Critique

I examined the sole page at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480. The dragon, eclipse and receiving gate make a clear triangle, and the river carries the eye back from the near bank. The new foothill overlaps and stepped landing give the image convincing depth. The narrow viewport makes the eclipse look taller and compresses the castle openings. The wide viewport exposes the hard angles in the river bends and the broad, nearly empty block of yellow sky behind the fire.

The warm sky sets off the cool foreground and the heavy wing silhouette. The fire loses contrast on the brightest part of its descent, and the corona's lower rim and the stream nearly touch visually. The longest warm bands still make the upper sky look striped. At the bottom, the mauve reflection has straight, hard interior edges and the evenly fine dashes resemble texture laid on a road. The left and right forest silhouettes frame the river well.

The picture uses NAPLPS filled polygons well where one shape overlaps another, especially in the wing ribs, foothills and landing foundations. The native arc for the suspended coal and the tiny line and point accents stay crisp. The river's flat bands and the towers' simple triangular roofs leave room for more expressive use of the same primitives.

The scene fits high fantasy through the dragon, wooded mountain basin and fire-handling citadel. The tithe and its split-spire apparatus give the world an invented ritual with a visible destination. The ordinary pointed roofs on either side are the most generic part of that architecture. At 480 × 460 the dragon's eye, fire and arch remain identifiable. The page contains no visible title, text, labels, numerals or credits at either size.

## Priorities

1. Break up the sky bands and put a quieter, darker sky behind the descending fire.
2. Round the river bends and use grouped ripples to break up the solid reflection.
3. Give the two outer towers open lantern crowns shaped for the citadel's fire ritual.

## Changes and checks

- Added overlapping cloud bodies and lowered the opening of bright horizon sky behind the fire. The ribbon holds its warm edge through more of the descent, though its start still grazes the eclipse glow.
- Rounded the riverbanks and reflection polygons, darkened the broad water fill, and grouped the brighter crests with gaps between them. The shared random sequence also shifted the foreground tree placement.
- Replaced the outer pointed roofs with open stone crowns and lit lanterns. Both flames are visible at 480 × 460, and the central suspended coal still anchors the keep.
- The generator and validator already had no stream-size ceiling. The manifest records the regenerated stream's 19,502 bytes. Rebuilt the stream, both PNGs, contact sheet and manifest, then ran the standalone validator. Determinism, full RESET, 7-bit encoding, all 16 colour definitions, clean rendering and artifact agreement passed.
