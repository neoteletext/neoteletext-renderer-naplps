# Round 5 · The Ember Tithe

## Before

The single page at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480 has a clear sweep from the dragon's wings through its fire to the gate, then back down the river. The eclipse and citadel stack well on the right, though the wing tips nearly merge with the dark streaks behind them. Warm peach and apricot establish a convincing light source against the plum shadows. The new wooded terraces help with scale, but the range between the bright sky and the trees is mostly a row of blunt, unmodelled pink triangles. The river describes depth more convincingly than the peaks do.

The bold fills, narrow palette and sharp silhouettes suit NAPLPS. Relative polygons make the overlapping strata and wings possible, and the sparse point sparks and horizontal water strokes hold without clutter. The dragon's far wing reads as a narrow splinter with a largely empty span, and its nearest foreleg looks like a zigzag hanging from the throat. The fire thins into a smooth pale cord for much of the flight, especially at 480 × 460, and its loop largely disappears against the bright arch. The stepped rock and castle read clearly at both sizes. The eclipse remains slightly tall at 480 × 460 and slightly wide at 640 × 480.

The single scene fits high fantasy through the eclipse, dragon and remote citadel. The split keep cradling a coal and the dragon delivering fire give these familiar motifs an invented ritual and a purpose. The scene has no visible letters, numerals, labels or captions at either size. There is no text to decipher at 480 × 460.

## Priorities

1. Give the dragon a load-bearing far wing with connected finger bones and membranes, and make the folded foreleg and hooked talons readable.
2. Make the airborne fire visibly twist and break into embers, with a distinct flare as it enters the gate.
3. Give the distant range lit rock or snow faces and shaded gullies so it reads as terrain at a different scale from the wooded terraces.

## Changes and checks

- Rebuilt the far wing with a wider silhouette, connected ribs and separate membrane panels. Redrew the folded foreleg with a lit underside and three hooked talons.
- Added loose flame tongues and ember streaks along the ribbon, shaded the middle bend for contrast against the peach sky, and added an impact flare within the receiving arch.
- Added warm snow faces and shaded gullies to the distant peaks. The main summit still sits partly behind the dragon's tail at both sizes.
- Confirmed the generator and validator already have no byte-size ceiling. Rebuilt the 17,333-byte stream, both PNGs, the contact sheet and the manifest. The standalone validator passed its deterministic generation, RESET, 7-bit, palette, clean render and output agreement checks.
- Inspected both final renders and the contact sheet and archived them with this round. The scene remains wordless.
