# Round 11 · The Ember Tithe

## Critique

I inspected the only page, 176, at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480. The dragon's wings balance the eclipse and keep, and the river's S-curve takes the eye back into the valley. The eclipse is almost round at 640 × 480 and conspicuously tall at 480 × 460. Its halo reads as stacked outlines whose edges barely relate to the wind-sheared sky. The warm fire against plum stone anchors the right side, but the receiving arch and the ascending flume look like separate bright marks. The landing underneath reads as a thin shelf with dangling fragments. The distant ridges and dark wooded banks establish depth, although some terraces turn into rows of teeth and the foreground reflection is crowded with horizontal dashes.

NAPLPS works best here through overlapping filled silhouettes, a small colour map and relative polygons that describe the wings, cliffs and cloud banks. The fine points and line strokes add sparks and ripples. In the smaller render those strokes offer too little help where the architecture and the fire route need to read as connected shapes.

The scene meets the high-fantasy theme as one inhabited landscape with a single action, from dragon to receiving station to the coal above the keep. That ritual and the forked lantern towers give the invented world its own identity. The batlike wing silhouette and conifer valley are familiar fantasy vocabulary. The exchange of eclipse fire is the original idea and deserves a clearer physical path through the building.

At 480 × 460 the image is wordless. There are no visible labels, letters, numerals or credits. “The Ember Tithe” is the manifest title and is absent from both renders.

## Priorities

1. Share the eclipse's aspect distortion more evenly between the two render sizes, and carry its outer light into the surrounding cloud on the downwind side.
2. Give the strike a visible route over the receiving sill and into the flume, with a projecting stone channel and supports that carry the terrace into the cliff.

## Changes made

- Widened the eclipse and extended its outer light into the cloud bank to the right. The disc now reads closer to round at 480 × 460 and a little wider at 640 × 480. Both renders use the same stream.
- Ran a stone-lined fire channel from the strike inside the arch across the sill, into the existing flume and up toward the suspended coal. Rebuilt the terrace supports as broad masonry faces keyed into the cliff.
- Audited the generator and validator for stream size ceilings. Neither contained one. The manifest still records the byte count, and the validator still enforces 7-bit encoding.
- Regenerated the stream, both PNGs, the contact sheet and the manifest. Inspected both new renders and saved them with the contact sheet in the round 11 archive. Both renders remain wordless. The validator passed for the full reset, all 16 colour definitions, 7-bit encoding and both render sizes.
