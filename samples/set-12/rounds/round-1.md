# Round 1 · The Ember Tithe

## Before

I inspected page 176 at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480. The river leads the eye into the valley, and the dragon's left wing and tail balance the citadel on the right. The nesting of dark banks, plum ridges and coral peaks gives the scene real depth. The eclipse is the strongest visual element, but its broad concentric rings look like a target and compete with the dragon and keep. At 480 × 460 the eclipse stretches into a pronounced oval, and the tightly spaced roof tips compete with its lower edge. The sky and ground share so many broad, hard-edged bands that they begin to look like the same material.

The native filled arcs, shared 16-colour map and faceted polygons suit NAPLPS well. The polygon work is strongest in the dragon's silhouette and weaker in the scenery, where straight edges and repeating triangle shapes dominate. The river looks like a zigzag ribbon with a few stray scratches. The banks are rows of nearly identical arrowheads, and the cliff is a dark block with three parallel stripes. The castle reads as a bundle of pencil towers. Its thin windows and tiny gate offer little sense of scale, and the dragon's scattered sparks barely connect its jaw with their destination.

The page clearly delivers one high-fantasy scene. Mountains, a dragon and a spired keep are familiar ingredients. The specific idea of a dragon paying an eclipse-fire tithe gives them an original relationship, but the picture only hints at that ritual. At 480 × 460 there is no in-picture text to test for legibility. The title exists in the supporting material.

## Priorities

1. Give the valley more depth and texture through sky haze, water reflections and irregular, tiered fir silhouettes.
2. Give the citadel a terraced structure, a prominent receiving gate and stonework that follows the shape of the cliff.
3. Make the dragon's anatomy and the falling fire clear enough to tell the story at both sizes.

## Changes and checks

- Softened the outer eclipse halo and added frayed cloud wisps. Broke the river reflection into short ripples contained by its banks and gave the firs irregular tiers, spacing and lean.
- Built a lower receiving terrace with a lit arch, steps and a buttress. Added arched windows and wall openings, and traced fractures down the cliff faces.
- Gave the dragon lit wing bones, shoulder and throat facets, and a visible eye. Curved the glowing coals and their wake into the receiving arch, with the nearest turret clear of their path.
- Removed the validator's 6,000-byte ceiling. The generator had no separate ceiling. The 9,773-byte stream passed validation for deterministic generation, 7-bit encoding, full RESET, the 16-colour map and clean rendering. Regenerated the stream, both PNGs, the contact sheet and the manifest.
- Rechecked both final renders and the contact sheet. The scene stays wordless. At 480 × 460 the eclipse is still vertically stretched by the renderer's viewport mapping, while the dragon, ember trail and receiving arch remain distinct.
