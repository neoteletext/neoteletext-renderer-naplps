# Round 4 · The Ember Tithe

## Before

I inspected the single page at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480. The dragon, fire and gate give the eye a clear route through the upper half, and the river leads back into the scene from below. The cliff appears suspended over an almost empty, dark valley floor. The foreground firs then arrive as a near-solid border, with little between them and the distant mountains to establish scale.

The cool plum shadows and warm peach light work together. The contrast around the dragon's head and the receiving arch holds at both sizes. The sky's broad diagonal bands are so clean and continuous that they look like flat stripes. The corona's nested rims make it look like a scalloped medallion, and its tall oval at 480 × 460 makes that impression stronger. The coal above the keep is clear, but it appears unrelated to the fire entering below it. The far wing and foreclaw still read more as angular silhouettes than as articulated forms.

The clean silhouettes, limited palette, relative polygons and precisely placed sparks and wave crests play to NAPLPS's strengths. The native arc renders cleanly, although its aspect changes with the viewport. The scene fits high fantasy through its dragon, eclipse and isolated citadel. The invented collection of eclipse fire gives the familiar dragon and castle motifs a specific purpose, but the visible action stops at the doorway. At 480 × 460 there are no letters, numerals, captions or other text in the image. The wordless composition is deliberate.

## Priorities

1. Break up the planar sky bands with wisps and shadow, and balance the eclipse proportions across the two viewports.
2. Add wooded middle-distance terraces and low mist so the foreground, river and cliff share a continuous landscape.
3. Carry light from the receiving arch along the masonry toward the suspended coal so the keep visibly participates in the tithe.

## Changes and checks

- Added wind-sheared cloud banks across the sky bands and rebuilt the eclipse with layered polygon plumes and a thinner rim. Its single outline is slightly tall at 480 × 460 and slightly wide at 640 × 480.
- Added wooded terraces at several depths on both banks, with low mist between the stands. Shaped the feet of the terraces into the slopes so the new silhouettes follow the terrain.
- Cut a glowing channel into the keep and added rising flame and sparks below the coal. The channel is visible at both sizes, while the smallest sparks are subtle at 480 × 460.
- Confirmed that the generator and validator already had no stream-size ceiling. Rebuilt the 16,749-byte stream, both PNGs, the contact sheet and the manifest. Ran the validator again to confirm deterministic generation, 7-bit encoding, the full reset, all 16 palette entries, clean rendering and agreement with the generated files.
- Inspected both final native-size renders and the contact sheet, and archived them with this round. The picture is wordless.
