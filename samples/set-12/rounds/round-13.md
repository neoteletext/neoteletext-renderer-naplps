# Round 13 · The Ember Tithe

## Critique

I inspected page 176, the set's only page, at native 480 × 460 and 640 × 480. The dragon and eclipse balance the upper frame, and the fire, keep and river form an easy route through the view. At 640 × 480 there is more breathing room around the wings and the river bends read more clearly. At 480 × 460 the eclipse grows tall and the gate and flume bunch together, though the main action remains readable. Peach and copper against plum and blue-black create a convincing progression into shadow. The lower corners are so compressed in value that the closest foliage largely disappears into the ground.

Filled shapes and the indexed palette make good use of NAPLPS. Overlapping rock beds, wing membranes and cloud banks establish depth without relying on fine marks. The solid arc at the heart of the cradle stays distinct at either size. The fire is the weak link. Its cream core and dark copper edge keep it legible, but the long midsection looks like one smooth cord. The strike has more energy than the fire travelling toward it, and the sparks in the middle are too small to change that impression at 480 × 460.

The image meets the high-fantasy brief as a single event in a large landscape. The receiving arch and the coal held between the spires give the ritual a destination and suggest a built world with its own practices. The eclipse tithe is the most original element. The nearest rows of uniformly pointed firs are the most generic. They resemble a stock framing device, and their nearly solid silhouettes make the foreground feel less finished than the middle distance.

There is no text at 480 × 460. The artwork contains no title, lettering, numerals, labels or credits. The title is in the manifest.

## Priorities

1. Give the eclipse fire a visible twist through the open sky with alternating overlaps, open gaps and a concentrated flare at the gate.
2. Replace the nearest fir silhouettes with fork-crowned trees whose branch structure and faint reflected edges read against the dark banks.

## Changes made

- Added a copper return strand behind the cream core and brought a nearer fold across it before the gate. The openings between strands show the twist in both renders. Both folds converge on the existing strike.
- Built the nearest trees from forked trunks, swept crowns and shaded facets. Opened gaps between the closest groups so their silhouettes and the exposed banks are visible at 480 × 460.
- Confirmed the generator and validator already accept streams without a byte ceiling. The byte count is reported for reference.
- Regenerated the stream, both native PNGs, contact sheet and manifest. Inspected the finished renders and contact sheet, refreshed the round 13 preview archive and ran `validate.py` successfully. The picture remains wordless, and validation confirmed the 7-bit stream, full reset and all 16 palette entries.
