# Science-fiction pictures · 166–175

[Contact sheet](out/contact-sheet.png) · [Manifest](out/manifest.json) · [Concepts](CONCEPTS.md)

Each picture starts with a full reset, defines all sixteen colour-map entries and uses a single high-contrast title in the repository stroke font. The contact sheet groups pages 166–170 and 171–175. Within each group the 640 × 480 renders sit above the 480 × 460 renders. Both rows come from direct renders at the named dimensions, shown at half size in the sheet.

Run `python3 set-11/validate.py --build` from `samples/` to regenerate the streams, PNGs, sheet and manifest. Run `python3 set-11/validate.py` to verify reproducibility, 7-bit framing and all sixteen palette entries, renderer diagnostics, both preview sizes and the two animated pages. Individual generators also write their own stream when run as scripts.

## 166 · Keelward

Fractures and salt seams expose the crust that supports the city. A supported transit line carries a lit carriage to the deep tower's station while a ferry crosses the gulf below.

## 167 · Cinder Mail

Faceted fabric, reinforcing ribs and separate stays give the sail its shape. Strands of ash part along the leading edge and rejoin behind the courier.

## 168 · Prism Grazer

Bright facets give way to violet and teal along the animal's underside. A split ray enters its lowered muzzle and refracts through the body. Exposed faces and coloured reflections break up the ground around the seam.

## 169 · The Second Floor

Nested frames lead to the two surveyors exchanging an instrument. Deck rails and opposing apricot and teal surfaces establish each surveyor's local sense of down.

## 170 · Umbra Loom

Hooked eruptions rise from the cropped star. The strands in each ribbon cross as they enter the shuttle guides. BLINK changes the central filament in each ribbon on the same pulse.

## 171 · Rainworks

Copper tanks of different sizes feed five outlets. The centre outlet releases a continuous jet as the others send drops down staggered paths into the catchment. WAIT holds the charged vessels for one second before the discharge. A relative macro draws each drop.

## 172 · Choir of Ice

Reeds of varying heights stand on separate ledges around a foreground soloist. Native hatch covers the shaded faces of the nearer reeds. The arcs of the three sounding reeds meet in the space above the choir.

## 173 · Orchard in Transit

Two drives push a ribbed freight capsule across an ice moon. Saplings grow along a shared planting bed fed by a pipe that runs below the window.

## 174 · Borrowed Dawn

An articulated tower carries the hinged reflector that redirects sunlight into the valley. The beam fans across the slope and lights terraced streets, lamps and rooftops.

## 175 · The Truce Line

Two differently jointed machines lower open hands beside planted banners. G3 mosaic dashes widen toward the foreground between exposed shelves on the floor of a white sea.
