# Set 15 · Improvement round 4

## Critique of the round-3 renders

### 197 · The Electronic Front Page · 2,363 bytes

The page is complete in structure. The index keycaps at 12 × 18 fixed pitch are the thinnest text on the page at 480 × 460. The lead picture's sky is a flat field above the bridge, and the LATE line gives no sense of the paper being kept up to date through the morning.

- Keycap names in 14 × 20 proportional type, numbers in 13 × 20.
- A time stamp on the late item.
- Clouds and two of the paper's gulls over the harbour.

### 198 · Weather Map of the Westmark · 2,124 bytes

A complete forecast page. The towns float unconnected on the land; the bay is empty; the cold front's story has no wind behind it.

- The highway between the towns as a dotted line.
- Two islands in Kestrel Bay.
- A wind arrow and "NW 40" behind the front (moved above the arrow when the first placement crossed the front at 480 × 460).

### 199 · Storm Warning · 1,440 bytes

The bulletin names two counties but a viewer cannot see where they are; the upper-left sky is empty cloud.

- An inset map of the two counties, cross-hatched in red, with their seats as dots, framed in cream.

### 200 · Market Close · 3,039 bytes

The chart shows the day's move but not against what: the change in the headline is from yesterday's close, which the chart does not show.

- Yesterday's close as a dotted cyan reference line across the chart, labelled PREV.

### 201 · Last Night's Scores · 1,965 bytes

The line score appears at once, so the page's build tells nothing of the game.

- The line score replayed inning by inning, a WAIT before each inning and a longer one before the tenth; totals last.

### 202 · The Community Almanac · 2,479 bytes

The page has no foot: it ends in empty paper, without the "where next" every videotex page carried; the legend chips run into the paper.

- A teal footer with MORE TABLES and TOWN MAP page numbers.
- Legend chips outlined in ink; the isotype key lifted clear of the footer.

### 203 · Ask the Encyclopedia: Volcanoes · 1,827 bytes

The strata are seven evenly spaced chevrons, too regular for deposits of separate eruptions. The side vent's dike stops inside the cone, so it vents nothing.

- Eight strata of uneven thickness and bend.
- The dike carried to the flank, with a small cone of its own feeding the flow.

### 204 · City Directory: Hotels · 2,131 bytes

The city ends in a hard edge above the card, though it is Calder *Bay*; the whole table arrives at once.

- The bay in front of the city, with broken reflection streaks drawn in the window entry, so they light with the windows when the colour map changes.
- A WAIT before each directory row.

### 205 · Your Own Newspaper · 2,588 bytes

Each folder is a dead end: nothing says where to read more. The school icon is a building alone, though the item is a concert.

- A page number at the foot of every folder (the NEW tag moved left of it).
- Two children singing beside the school.

### 206 · The Night Sky Tonight · 2,865 bytes

The page shows night from the first byte, though the colour map can make the evening happen.

- The dome opens in twilight colours with the houses against it; after a WAIT its four entries are turned to night, and only then do the Milky Way, stars, Moon and planets appear. The roofline is drawn again over the stars.
