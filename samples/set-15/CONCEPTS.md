# Set 15 · Videotex as its makers dreamed it: news, weather and information · 197–206

The ten concepts of group C of the batch's research (concepts 21 to 30): the newspaper, the weather, the markets, sports, statistics, the encyclopedia, the city directory, personal news and the night sky, as Telidon and NAPLPS were promoted to deliver them to the living-room television around 1980 to 1985. Every service, company, place and person is invented. Most pages belong to one invented city, Calder Bay, and its region, the Westmark, so that the pages refer to one another by page number as a service's pages did.

## Changes from the research's concepts

- **21 → 197.** The paper is the *Calder Bay Courier*, not the *Daily Courier*, since several real newspapers carry that name. The capitol-dome icon became the lead picture: city hall above a harbour across which the approved bridge is drawn dashed, after a pause, as THE PLAN; the ballpark and rocket stories are kept. A keycap index ties the page to the rest of the set.
- **22 → 198.** "The Prairies" became an invented region, the Westmark, with invented towns, a bay and islands, so that no real province is outlined.
- **24 → 200.** *Bayview Securities* became *Quillon & Marsh*, to avoid any real firm's name; the ten shares are invented.
- **26 → 202.** The bar chart of population became an isotype chart, rows of one programmable figure per thousand people, which is both more of its period and a better use of DRCS and REPEAT.
- **27 → 203.** *Lumen Electronic Encyclopedia* became the *Quarto Home Encyclopedia*.
- **29 → 205.** The reader is Edna Pellow and her paper *The Pellow Post*; her topics are gardening, curling, a stock club and her grandchild's school.
