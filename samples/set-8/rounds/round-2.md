# Set 8 · Improvement round 2

## Critique and priorities

I examined the individual 480 × 460 and 640 × 480 renders before editing. The text assessments below refer to the 480 × 460 images.

### 136 · Light That Stays

The interface provides a strong horizontal division and cyan and amber make the two cases easy to compare. At both sizes the partial reflection looks like construction geometry until the leader catches the eye. AIR and GLASS sit on the far left, while CRITICAL: 42 DEG hangs over the right example as if it applies only there. The title, TOTAL INTERNAL REFLECTION, AIR, GLASS, CRITICAL: 42 DEG, PARTIAL, 25 DEG / BELOW and 44 DEG / ABOVE all read. The familiar ray plate fits optics squarely. The staged reveal and indexed arrows are the distinctive parts, but the two outgoing mechanisms are still underspecified.

**Priorities** — Give the critical angle a shared position. Label the refracted and reflected directions and connect the angle values to their arcs.

### 137 · The Still Points

Three balanced lobes dominate the composition. Lime nodes pop, but the wider image makes clear how much empty space the lower leaders occupy and how faint the second extreme is. The title, THIRD HARMONIC, MAXIMUM MOTION, both FIXED and both NODE labels, TWO EXTREMES and REST POSITION all read. MAXIMUM MOTION points to one extremum, and both extremes have only one legend swatch. It suits physics well and palette BLINK is a more interesting use of NAPLPS than a static textbook trace.

**Priorities** — Name the antinodes and show their full travel on all three lobes. Make both extremes legible together while retaining the blink.

### 138 · Salt in Three Dimensions

The warm-to-cool split frames the ion model and its key. Teal and coral hold up well, but the full grid makes the lattice look like furniture. The inset's spokes and cage edges merge at both sizes, and there is no indication which Na+ site the inset expands. Both title lines, ROCK SALT, Na+, Cl- and both lines of 6 NEAREST NEIGHBOURS read. Depth-ordered discs are effective native primitives. The paired views make this more informative than a stock ball-and-stick picture and fit structural chemistry.

**Priorities** — Highlight the central sodium and its neighbours in the unit cell. Give the coordination inset a shaded octahedral surface and a clear hierarchy of edges.

### 139 · Ink in Flight

The warm paper and dark sidebar have good contrast, but the generic sidebar explanation uses almost as much space as the experiment. Each band's nested oval is conspicuously regular. The title, PAPER / CHROMATOGRAPHY, DYES TRAVEL / AT DIFFERENT / RATES, SOLVENT / FRONT, FLOW, ORIGIN, BATH, START and DEVELOPED all read. WAIT makes the comparison effective, although the bath hatch adds little and the plate lacks the measured distances that distinguish chromatography from a generic separation sketch. Strong chemistry fit, with originality mainly in the staggered reveal.

**Priorities** — Add a quantitative solvent-travel scale tied to the actual spot positions. Draw irregular, layered dye deposits with vertical tailing.

### 140 · A Leaf Breathes

The pale green pair is luminous and the lobed seams give the field a strong microscope character. The title, GUARD CELLS and OPEN PORE are clear. At both sizes the guard cells look like a single oval outline split by a black slit, and the labels identify a static object while the title promises a process. The traced shared walls and contour cutout suit NAPLPS. A direct comparison would make this biology plate more distinctive and explain what the viewer is seeing.

**Priorities** — Compare the same guard-cell anatomy in open and closed states. Make the two cells distinct and link the change in pore width to guard-cell turgor.

### 141 · One Cell Becomes Two

The rose chromosomes remain distinct from the lilac spindle and plum panels. The title, MITOSIS IN AN ANIMAL CELL, 1–4 and the four stage names all read. The two arrows are clear in isolation, but there is no equivalent cue from stage 2 to 3. At both sizes the straight fibres and evenly spaced chromosomes look schematic, and in the final panel four separate chromosomes nearly form a single X. Animal-cell division fits the theme, and the fluorescence palette and relative chromosome macro give a familiar sequence some character.

**Priorities** — Use a continuous reading path through all stages. Show centromere-first separation and keep the four chromosomes distinct within each daughter nucleus.

### 142 · A Star Winks

The amber star and cyan trace have strong contrast. The aligned composition gives a familiar astronomy subject an original quantitative treatment, and native disc overpainting plus a computed polyline work well. The title, EXOPLANET TRANSIT, PLANET POSITIONS, RELATIVE LIGHT, 100%, 97.44%, 2.56% DIP and TIME all read. The two points per limb are almost touching at channel size, and the selected points fall near the slope without identifying the actual contact events.

**Priorities** — Plot and number the four exact contacts. Give the ingress and egress enough width for the numbered guides and four curve markers to read clearly.

### 143 · The Ocean Keeps a Record

The muted rust and blue let the block dominate at both sizes. The matching widths carry the geological idea and native faceting gives it real depth. Both title lines, MAGNETIC STRIPES, RIDGE AXIS, NEW BASALT, NORMAL and REVERSED are legible. The broad brown front face and triangular wedge leave the creation and motion of crust ambiguous. The archival metaphor is distinctive, but the actual bands still look like stripes painted across a roof.

**Priorities** — Make the cross-section explain spreading, with a labeled rising melt zone and arrows attached to the crust. Show that matching bands have the same age.

### 144 · Stone That Shrinks

The sunset palette sets off the basalt, and the planar faces have the emphatic contrast NAPLPS handles well. The title, COLUMNAR BASALT, TOP VIEW, JOINTS and COOLING LAVA CONTRACTS AND CRACKS all read. The cliff still looks like separate pencils with slightly offset caps, while the inset is a perfect honeycomb. The zigzag leader is nearly as dominant as the fractures it points to. The subject is apt and the cliff-plus-plan pairing has potential if the two views actually correspond.

**Priorities** — Construct both views from one irregular shared-edge joint network. Show how the vertical joints relate to the cooling face and make the inset a direct reading of the cliff.

### 145 · A Square Moves

The squares balance and the four triangle colours are distinct. The title, PYTHAGORAS BY REARRANGEMENT, A–D in both squares, a and b, all three squared-area labels, SAME TRIANGLES. SAME AREA. and the equation read. The blue areas and WAIT make the classic proof easy to stage with NAPLPS polygons. The proof is familiar, and the presentation tracks the pieces well, but the bare side labels do not establish both outer dimensions. The caption can also be read as saying the triangles themselves are the areas being equated.

**Priorities** — Show the shared outer side lengths and explicitly mark the right triangles. State the equality in terms of the uncovered area.

## Changes made

- **136 · Light That Stays** — Put the shared glass-to-air critical angle above the comparison. Placed explicit labels for refraction, partial reflection and total reflection next to their paths. Kept the incidence angles with the corresponding colour and the WAIT reveal.
- **137 · The Still Points** — Named the antinodes and drew opposing travel arrows at all three. Increased the dim profile's contrast and keyed both phases separately while keeping the palette blink.
- **138 · Salt in Three Dimensions** — Highlighted the central Na+ and its six Cl- neighbours with gold rings and links. Faded the general grid and shaded the octahedral inset to distinguish its faces and rear site.
- **139 · Ink in Flight** — Calculated each dye's fraction of solvent travel from the plotted origin, front and centre ticks. Replaced uniform ellipses with layered deposits that trail toward the origin.
- **140 · A Leaf Breathes** — Rebuilt the plate as open and closed views on a shared scale. Drew separate guard-cell contours and identified the corresponding high and low turgor states.
- **141 · One Cell Becomes Two** — Arranged the numbered stages clockwise and connected every transition. Widened the chromosome separation, clarified the four individual marks in each daughter nucleus and marked the constricting furrow.
- **142 · A Star Winks** — Computed and numbered the four exact contacts and aligned them with the corresponding light-curve points. Enlarged the star and transit span to make ingress and egress discernible.
- **143 · The Ocean Keeps a Record** — Cut a shallow trough at the axis, showed rising melt and labeled outward spreading in the section. Matched two equal-age points in the same reversal sequence using their model coordinates.
- **144 · Stone That Shrinks** — Built the cliff and its plan from one irregular shared-edge mesh and highlighted the same column in both. Added the cooling-face and joint-growth annotations.
- **145 · A Square Moves** — Bracketed the equal outer sides, marked all eight right angles and labeled the hypotenuse. Made the final comparison explicitly about the uncovered areas.
