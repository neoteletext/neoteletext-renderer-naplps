# Science in NAPLPS · 136–145

[Contact sheet](out/contact-sheet.png) · [Manifest](out/manifest.json) · [Concepts](CONCEPTS.md)

Each group of five on the contact sheet shows the 640 × 480 previews above the 480 × 460 previews. The individual PNGs are rendered directly at their named sizes with the repository's stroke font. The normalized viewport stretches circles vertically on the channel raster. Angles and lengths in geometric diagrams are constructed in the 640 × 480 design space.

Run `python3 validate.py` from this directory to regenerate in a temporary directory, verify the reset, 16-entry colour map, determinism and 7-bit encoding, and compare the streams and both renders with the delivered files. Run `python3 validate.py --write` to rebuild all outputs. Each page script can also write its own stream directly. Stream byte counts in the manifest are informational. The three WAIT pages can be inspected with the renderer's `--stage 1` option. The standing-wave page alternates the emphasized extreme every six tenths of a second.

## 136 · Light That Stays

The incidence angles round to 25° and 44°. For glass with refractive index 1.5 against air, the critical angle rounds to 42°. WAIT reveals the 39° refracted ray and a weaker partial reflection on the cyan side, and total internal reflection on the amber side.

## 137 · The Still Points

The two extreme profiles sample the third spatial harmonic of a fixed string. Palette BLINK changes their emphasis. The rest line, four stationary nodes and three pairs of antinode travel arrows stay fixed. A bracket spans one full wavelength.

## 138 · Salt in Three Dimensions

The oblique three-by-three-by-three grid shows chloride at the corners and face centres. Gold links and rings identify the six chloride neighbours of the central sodium ion. The shaded inset uses dashed rear edges to open a view through the coordinating octahedron.

## 139 · Ink in Flight

WAIT pairs the original spot with the developed chromatogram. The baseline sits above the bath, and layered pigment contours mark three unequal distances below the solvent front. Each labelled fraction compares dye travel with solvent travel from the same origin.

## 140 · A Leaf Breathes

Lobed pavement-cell seams surround paired views of a generic stoma with labelled guard cell and nucleus. The open and closed states contrast guard-cell shape and turgor. Smaller bodies suggest chloroplasts and the larger ovals depict the nuclei.

## 141 · One Cell Becomes Two

Four numbered fields connected clockwise by arrows show mitosis in an animal cell with four chromosomes. A relative macro draws the duplicated chromosomes. The spindle poles are gold. Chromosome colours track the four original chromosomes into both re-forming nuclei.

## 142 · A Star Winks

Four snapshots of one planet align the geometric contacts with bracketed ingress and egress intervals on the computed light curve. The model assumes constant projected speed, uniform stellar brightness and a planet with 16% of the star's radius. The dip is 2.56% and the flux axis displays the range around it.

## 143 · The Ocean Keeps a Record

Mirrored polarity widths record reversals on either side of the ridge. Short strokes identify reversed bands. The cutaway shows rising melt beneath the axis and arrows indicate outward spreading. Leaders pick out a matching pair of bands.

## 144 · Stone That Shrinks

The cliff and plan inset project the same irregular polygonal joint network. A growth arrow runs down perpendicular to the illustrated cooling surface, and shaded faces distinguish vertical joints from cross-fractures.

## 145 · A Square Moves

Dimension brackets identify the equal outer side lengths, and small squares mark the right angles. WAIT reveals the second arrangement, where the same four congruent triangles are tracked by colour and letter. Comparing the remaining areas gives the Pythagorean identity.
