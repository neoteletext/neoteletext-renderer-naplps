# Set 16 concepts: work, farming, education, travel and government (pages 207 to 216)

The theme is videotex as its makers and its period imagined it. These ten are the uses that were promised
outside the living room: the farm office, the airfield, the school, the travel agent, the town hall, the
spare-bedroom office and the clinic. Seven are screens of invented services, as a subscriber would have seen
them; three are scenes of the people the services were for.

Every service, company, place and person is invented. Telephone numbers use the 555-01xx range kept for fiction.

## Changes to the concepts as given

- **211:** the brief's lesson turned the orange slices green on a right answer, which leaves a still that asks
  "what fraction is orange?" of a pie with no orange in it. Here the slices stay orange; the answer's key turns
  green by a change of the colour map, and the three slices flash so that they can be counted.
- **208:** the day is given as five named hours across the screen, each with its own verdict colour, so that the
  one-line verdict at the foot can be checked against the figures above it.
- **210:** the cloud is drawn as a deck over part of the chart as well as by the colour of each airfield.
- **210:** the page belongs to the airfield whose terminal it is on, Corbel Lake, not to a named briefing
  service: the first title chosen for it proved to be the name of a real product and was dropped in round 5.
- **207:** the elevator's town was first given a name that a real town has; it is now Halven.
- The invented names differ from the research note's where a note's name was plain enough that a real service
  might share it: Furrowline, Corbel Lake, Chalkwire, Sundrift Holidays, Elderwick, Tamarack Creek.
