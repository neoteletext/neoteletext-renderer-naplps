# Round 4

Looked at every page's round-3 renders at both sizes, and at the contact sheet for the set as a whole.

## Critique

**The foot bands (207, 208, 213, 216).** "1 FUTURES  2 WEATHER  0 INDEX" is a line of text; on a service
worked from a keypad the digits are keys. Change: the kit draws each digit on a key top (`keycap`, `menu`).

**207 Grain Prices at the Elevator.** Rich now, and the stubble pattern holds the foreground. The elevator's
lit faces are flat red-brown slabs, and nothing leads the eye from the bottom of the picture to the elevator.
Changes: (1) courses of cribbed timber on the lit faces; (2) a wire fence coming forward down the left, posts
growing as they near; (3) key tops.

**208 Spraying Weather.** The DRIFT row is what a farmer wants. The horizon is three dark lumps; the windsock
hangs limp all day though the wind triples. Changes: (1) a shelterbelt, a barn with its silo and three grain
bins on the horizon; (2) the windsock moved to a tall pole clear of the storm, hanging at first, then painted
out and redrawn streaming when the storm is drawn; (3) key tops.

**209 The Farm Kitchen.** Warm and readable. The top right corner is empty, the range is a white box, and
dawn has no herald. Changes: (1) a pendant lamp and a shelf of three enamel canisters; (2) a checked tea towel
on the oven rail; (3) a rooster on the yard fence against the sunrise.

**210 Flight Briefing.** The route story works. Water meets land without a line, every runway lies the same
way, and the uplands give no height. Changes: (1) shorelines and sandbanks in a fine light line; (2) each
field's runway on its own heading; (3) a spot height by the peaks. One barb moved off a plate; the spot height
first landed on a peak and was moved.

**211 Lesson Four: Fractions.** It teaches and rewards. A fraction is only shown one way, and the question
does not point at what it asks about. Changes: (1) after the count, the same fraction as a bar of eight parts,
three orange, drawn part by part; (2) a chalk arrow from the question to the pie; (3) brass screws in the
frame's corners.

**212 The Classroom Terminal.** Full of life. But the screen, the subject of the picture, is drawn early and
then just sits; the alphabet stops at E; the board has only words. Changes: (1) the scene is drawn first, then
after a WAIT the map draws itself, one land at a time, then the equator, and only then the pointer comes up;
(2) the alphabet carried on to J across the wall; (3) a chalk compass on the board. A first attempt dropped G
from the alphabet; fixed.

**213 Book Your Holiday.** Close to finished. The foot band is plain, and the beach has things but no people.
Changes: (1) key tops; (2) a sandcastle with a flag, and a swimmer past the surf; (3) the palms' shadows on
the sand (they fall mostly in the darker sand and barely show).

**214 Vote on the New Library.** A fault found on inspection: the blink was on the amber entry, which is also
the title and the crest, so the township's name flashed. The page also never shows where Mill Street is.
Changes: (1) a ring entry of its own (the window-glass entry given up; windows are slate), so only the ring
round the pressed key flashes; (2) an inset street plan with the lot marked in the ring's colour, flashing
with it; (3) a parent and child on the path; the right-hand trees removed for the plan and the flag moved.

**215 Working from Home.** The jokes land. The chart is simply there; the top of the wall is bare between
clock and shelf. Changes: (1) the chart is drawn last with WAITs: five bars rise, the trend is drawn across
them, the cursor settles after the last figure; (2) a spider plant in a hanger; (3) slippers on the parent,
and a briefcase by the drawers. A jar of pencils was tried in two places and removed: both hid behind the
figure.

**216 The Community Health Page.** Complete as a service page. The road is empty and the foot band plain.
Changes: (1) key tops; (2) the town bus at its stop outside the clinic; (3) today's hours underlined on the
card. Ringing arcs at the handset were tried and removed: they ran into the N of NURSE.
