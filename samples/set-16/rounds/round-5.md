# Round 5

Looked at every page's round-4 renders at both sizes, and at intermediate stages of three animated pages
(`--stage`) to see that each stage stands as a picture.

## Critique

**207 Grain Prices at the Elevator.** The fence and the timber courses give the picture a foreground and a
surface. The train has cars and no engine, and the sunset ends without anything happening on the ground.
Changes: (1) the engine at the head of the train, black against the sun; (2) at the last step the yard light
comes on over the driveway and the evening star and two more come out; (3) nothing else: the board is done.

**208 Spraying Weather.** The sock tells the wind. The verdict names two hours without pointing at them, and
"drift" is a word on the board that the picture could show. Changes: (1) the two good hours bracketed in white
before the verdict is spelled out; (2) when the storm is drawn, every fan of spray gets a trail blown
downwind.

**209 The Farm Kitchen.** Nothing broken. The top of the range is a blank slab with a pan-shaped bar on it,
and the picture never shows what joins the box on the set to the world. Changes: (1) a proper pan with two
eggs; (2) a wall telephone with its coiled cord, and a red lead from its jack to the terminal box.

**210 Flight Briefing.** Dense but ordered. The spot height floats without a mark, and the map does not say
where the pilot is. Changes: (1) a height triangle beside the figure; (2) an aircraft symbol at the departure
field, nose along the route, drawn before the route. A name on the lake was tried and removed: it crowded the
plate beside it.

**211 Lesson Four: Fractions.** The bar is a second way of seeing the answer. The corner above the pie is
empty, and the lesson has no teacher. Changes: (1) an owl in a mortarboard in chalk outline, one wing out;
(2) when the answer is marked, a star appears on the end of the wing.

**212 The Classroom Terminal.** A fault found on inspection: round 4's alphabet fix had deleted the plant and
the coat pegs added in round 3. Restored (the plant's leaves now in the board's dark green, which shows against
the lawn). Also: nothing shows what the terminal is plugged into. Changes: (1) plant and pegs restored; (2) a
telephone jack by the bookcase with the terminal's lead running to it. An arm reaching to the keyboard was
tried and removed: it was a yellow plank across the cart.

**213 Book Your Holiday.** Finished in the main. Round 4's palm shadows fell in the darker sand and did not
show: removed. The hotel has no name, and the airliner is simply there. Changes: (1) the hotel named and given
three stars; (2) the airliner's trail grows piece by piece with WAITs while the panel is read, the aircraft
drawn at its head last; (3) the dead shadow code deleted.

**214 Vote on the New Library.** The plan and the ring work together. The wing windows are blank slabs and the
tally bar has no scale. Changes: (1) mullions and sills on the windows and a row of dentils under the cornice;
(2) a tick at every tenth under the bar.

**215 Working from Home.** The chart drawing itself is the best change of the set. The floor at the right is
empty; the parent's face is a disc with a triangle on it. Changes: (1) a wastebasket and the drafts that
missed it; (2) a fringe, a longer nose and a chin. A second car and a man hurrying for the bus were tried in
the window and removed: at that size they merged with the cat.

**216 The Community Health Page.** The page says a shot is due and offers a key, and then nothing happens.
Changes: (1) after a WAIT, key 1 is taken as pressed: the "DUE NOW" tab is replaced by "BOOKED: THU 2:30" in
the green of the open lamp, and the foot band's first choice becomes "CHANGE TIME"; (2) the lamp's blink now
goes to the lawn's green, so the booked tab pulses gently and its dark text stays readable in both states.
A first attempt repainted only part of the foot band and cut the start off "FIRST AID"; the whole band is now
redrawn.
