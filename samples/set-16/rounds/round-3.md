# Round 3

Looked at every page's round-2 renders at both sizes.

## Critique

**All six title bands.** A service's name overstruck for weight at 22 × 30 fills in the W of FURROWLINE and
ELDERWICK at 480 × 460. Change: a title size of 26 × 30, the same height and wider, on pages 207, 208, 210, 213,
214 and 216.

**207 Grain Prices at the Elevator.** The sunset by colour map is the page's event now. The field is a flat
gold with black wedges; the elevator is a silhouette without working parts; the three hopper cars are three
copies of one drawing sent three times. Changes: (1) the field given a defined stubble pattern; (2) a loading
spout out over the first car, a ladder, a lightning rod, birds on the wire; (3) the hopper car made a macro of
relative commands, called three times, with a rail and an open bay each.

**208 Spraying Weather.** Compass letters and the storm help. The field's stripes are one size from horizon to
foreground, so it reads as wallpaper; and the board says how hard the wind blows but not what that means for
spray. Changes: (1) the pattern defined twice, fine beyond and coarse close to, with the tractor's tramline
between; (2) a DRIFT row, LOW, SOME or HIGH in the verdict colour, and the gauges shortened to make room;
(3) row labels tidied.

**209 The Farm Kitchen.** The miniature of 207 on the set ties the two pages. The wall is one unbroken ochre;
the floor is empty round the chair; and nothing shows how he works the thing. Changes: (1) board wainscot to a
chair rail; (2) his left hand holds the keypad; (3) a rag rug under the chair and his boots by the stove.

**210 Flight Briefing.** Now it briefs. The cloud's lumps are a row of balls; a chart of weather has no wind;
the plates' second figure is unexplained. Changes: (1) scallops with shadows and fill-in lumps; (2) four wind
barbs, a feather for ten knots; (3) a small plate, "CEILINGS, FT". Two barbs first landed on plates and were
moved.

**211 Lesson Four: Fractions.** The stars scattered round "YES!" are decoration, and the word "orange" could
do what the page is teaching with. Changes: (1) a SCORE row under the keys, a star for each right answer and
rings for the questions to come; (2) "orange" written in orange; (3) grain on the frame and chalk dust on the
ledge. Smudges on the board were tried and removed: they looked like faults.

**212 The Classroom Terminal.** Fuller and livelier. The right of the room behind the teacher is blank wall,
the crowd stops short of her, and the map's white strip is unexplained. Changes: (1) a fifth child in front of
her, with a sailor collar; (2) coat pegs under the window, a plant on the sill, an apple on the bookcase;
(3) the strip under the map made a colour key.

**213 Book Your Holiday.** The shimmer is right for the medium. The price is simply there with everything
else; nothing urges; the sun does not touch the sea. Changes: (1) the price arrives after a WAIT and pops, a
small starburst then the full one; (2) "6 SEATS LEFT AT THIS PRICE"; (3) the sun's path on the water, cloud
over the island, and the parasol, chair and sail moved left so the path shows.

**214 Vote on the New Library.** The half-way mark completes the tally. The township has a name and no arms;
a vote cast leaves no trace the voter could quote; the trees are flat blobs. Changes: (1) a DRCS crest in the
title band; (2) a receipt number in the foot line, and the closing time moved to a plate on the picture;
(3) lighter crowns on the trees, a flag on a pole in the lawn, a bench with a reader.

**215 Working from Home.** The modem and cursor make the point. The child has blocks only; the window is a
picture with nobody looking at it; the desk top is bare beside the set. Changes: (1) a toy telephone and a
ball on the rug; (2) the cat on the sill, and folds in the curtains; (3) a jar of pencils. A mug put back at
her elbow read as a red mouth and was taken out again.

**216 The Community Health Page.** Personal and clear. The hours card says when the clinic is open but not
whether it is open now; the red band has a telephone and no person. Changes: (1) "OPEN NOW" with a green lamp
in an entry of its own, which blinks slowly (the road's grey entry given up for it; the road is now stone
colour); (2) a nurse with cap and headset at the right of the band.
