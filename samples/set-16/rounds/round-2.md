# Round 2

Looked at every page's round-1 renders, the 640 × 480 and the 480 × 460 side by side.

## Critique

**207 Grain Prices at the Elevator.** The board now informs, and the drawn decimal points settle the prices.
The trend lines are too flat to read as rising or falling at 480 × 460; the page uses none of the medium's
character sets or colour-map tricks; and the two farm pages have no mark that says they are one service.
Changes: (1) steeper trend lines over a floor line; (2) a DRCS head of grain before each grain's name and a DRCS
mark for the service; (3) the sky drawn in late-afternoon colours and carried down to sunset by the colour map,
one step with each row of prices.

**208 Spraying Weather.** The cards are a real gain. A wind arrow alone leaves a farmer to work out the
compass; the picture is the same from first byte to last although the forecast turns to rain; no service mark.
Changes: (1) compass letters with each speed, and bearings on the eight points; (2) a storm cloud and rain
streaks drawn into the picture when the forecast reaches noon; (3) the service's DRCS mark in the title band.

**209 The Farm Kitchen.** Much better composed. The strip of shadow under the cap reads as a bandage; the steam
crosses the calendar; and the screen shows a generic chart when this set has the very page he would be reading.
Changes: (1) grey hair at the temples instead of the strip; (2) steam moved clear of the calendar; (3) the
screen redrawn as page 207 in miniature: title band, elevator against the sky, four rows with arrows, the
rising arrows winking.

**210 Flight Briefing.** The plates carry the ceilings well. The dashed route is static and has no distances;
a chart has a scale and hazards; and the page does nothing with the one fact that matters, that the destination
is below limits. Changes: (1) the route drawn leg by leg with WAITs, each leg with its distance; (2) a
cross-hatched danger area and a scale bar; (3) after the destination flashes, an alternate leg in amber and a
fourth line telling the pilot which field to use. First attempt drew the route across the plates; the plates
now sit on the side away from the route (left, below, right).

**211 Lesson Four: Fractions.** The counting reveal teaches. The tick lies across the 8 of the right answer;
there is no sense of a lesson with a length; a right answer earns nothing. Changes: (1) fractions moved left in
their keys so the tick stands clear; (2) five progress pips, two done, the current one blinking in an entry of
its own; (3) chalk stars beside "YES!".

**212 The Classroom Terminal.** The teacher is a person now, but her neck is too long and her far arm is a
lump; the children are four copies of one trapezoid; the left of the room under the blackboard is empty.
Changes: (1) shoulders raised, the far arm folded round the lesson book; (2) each child given something of its
own: hood, striped jumper, braces, a satchel; (3) a bookcase and the classroom globe under the board, and the
map on the screen given Greenland and a dotted equator.

**213 Book Your Holiday.** The best of the set. The sea is still; the sand is bare; the service has no mark.
Changes: (1) the wave pattern's entry shimmers by BLINK (the flight labels moved to another entry so that they
do not); (2) a hut on stilts, a deck chair, a starfish and shells; (3) a DRCS half-sun-over-waves mark in the
title band.

**214 Vote on the New Library.** Clear. The picture does not say that this is a proposal and not a building;
the facade is plain; the tally has no mark for what carries the vote. Changes: (1) a plate, "THE PROPOSAL";
(2) an open book in the pediment, fluted columns, flower beds, clouds; (3) a half-way mark on the tally bar
with "HALF CARRIES IT".

**215 Working from Home.** The floor group is clear of the caption. A fault found on inspection: the blink was
on the yellow entry, which also colours the child's suit, a block and the car, so they would all have flashed.
The terminal's screen has no cursor; nothing shows how the terminal reaches the office. Changes: (1) the beige
entry given up for a lamp entry of its own: the cursor, the set's lamp and one modem lamp, which is what blinks;
(2) a modem under the telephone; (3) a child's crayon drawing taped low on the wall, and an axis and a heading
on the chart.

**216 The Community Health Page.** The personal timeline works. The bold row labels clog at 480 × 460; the
arrow under the ringed column points at nothing; the foot of the page offers nothing to do about the shot that
is due. Changes: (1) row labels unbolded; (2) the arrow replaced by a "DUE NOW" tab that flashes with the ring;
(3) key 1 now books the shot; a tree, a nurse at the door and a child with a balloon added to the picture.
