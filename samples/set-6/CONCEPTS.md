# Five NAPLPS pictures / 121–125

| Page | Title | Concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 121 | Afterimage | A mechanical iris hangs in a dark field. Lime light travels around its twelve violet blades in opposed pairs, with a matching six-position phase strip. |
| 122 | The Lure | A dive light sweeps empty water. An anglerfish enters the beam, then its lure lights up and reveals a row of teeth. |
| 123 | Fault Line | An imagined letterpress exhibition poster splits its headline across a diagonal fault. The two pieces of each letter slide past each other on bone and oxide-red paper. |
| 124 | Breathing Earth | A field-guide section through a termite mound shows a branching air shaft and underground chambers. A repeating stipple gives the exposed clay a granular surface. |
| 125 | Selvedge Moth | A symmetrical moth appears in a woven tile sampler. Square and triangular character masks form its scalloped wing edges, eyespots and seams. |
