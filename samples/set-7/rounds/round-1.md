# Set 7 · Improvement round 1

## Critique and priorities

I inspected each original PNG at its native 480 × 460 and 640 × 480 sizes. Text assessments below refer to the smaller render. The smaller aspect ratio makes circles taller and squeezes horizontal spacing, especially in the HUDs.

### 126 · Hem of the Sky

Cream type and the warm kite read immediately against blue. The sun's scanline cuts and the kite's broad polygon faces suit NAPLPS, but the identical upper faces blur together and the mountains are a generic zigzag hem. The title screen reads as a title screen, though the inventive premise is doing more work in the tagline than in the art. HEM OF / THE SKY, MEND THE WEATHER and PRESS START are all readable. The prompt sits high in its cream box.

Priorities: Show the actual tear and the process of repairing it. Give the kite a distinct light face and visible construction. Rebalance the start prompt and its animated arrow.

### 127 · Spore Switch

The pink stair-step of caps is a clear platforming composition. Dark plum keeps the bright ledges, green lamps and jump arc distinct, but the repeated blunt silhouettes look like cutouts suspended in an empty room. Relative macro reuse is appropriate and the arc gives an immediate sense of play. A beetle powering a fungal route is memorable, though the player currently reads as a green blob. SPORE SWITCH, LAMPS and CROWN LOG are readable. Unlit HUD circles are comparatively faint.

Priorities: Turn the flat room into a log interior with layered bark grain. Build up the platform undersides and ground the stems. Give the beetle a shell and make the unlit lamp state clearer.

### 128 · Siltfold

The cream-backed dashed route is easy to follow and the fill texture gives the basin a distinct surface without swallowing it. The four solid pools look like isolated blobs, and the broad blank parchment around them makes the map feel unfinished. Traversing land unlocked by low water is unusually specific game geography. SILTFOLD, LOW WATER, WEIR, FORD, LIFT and WALK TO THE LIFT are readable. The FORD patch interrupts the shoreline texture with a blunt rectangle.

Priorities: Draw recession contours and water shallows. Replace the rectangular patch with a sign tied to the ford marker. Give the endpoints recognizable weir and lift symbols.

### 129 · Tessera Vault

The left display case and strong amber selection divide the screen clearly. The solid mint, cream and ochre polygons are well suited to the format, but the relic's dark diamond looks flat on the floor and the pale outline rings in ECHO are thin and fragile. An inventory for trading physical properties is original. TESSERA VAULT, FALL UPWARD, WEIGHT 01, ECHO 02, HEAT 03, A EQUIP and B RETURN all read. RETURN is close to the right edge.

Priorities: Give the relic a visible gravity effect and add depth to its cavity. Make the three property icons equally deliberate and echo the selected relic's structure in its slot. Give the return control the same breathing room as equip.

### 130 · Brine Dash

Large numbers and slanted rows make a readable results board. The navy and persimmon are decisive, but the cream slashes look like arbitrary speed decoration and nearly disappear on the beige rows. The native polygons deliver a strong sports layout, though nothing depicts the invented sail-sled race. BRINE DASH, SALT FLAT FINAL, ranks 1–4, PELL 0:41.8, VOSK 0:42.2, EMRU 0:43.6, NALV 0:45.1 and NEW RECORD all read. The record tab touches the bottom edge.

Priorities: Build an identifiable sail-sled into each lane. Use the footer to show the margin over second place. Give the record tab a lower margin.

### 131 · Hillwalk Oath

The coral sun frames the dark hill cleanly, but the empty violet sky and blunt left bank leave the composition feeling like a stage with missing scenery. The flat silhouette and timed palette shift suit a cutscene. The premise is distinctive, though the revealed hill resembles a squat animal with clipped feet and spike-like trees. HILLWALK OATH and THEN THE HILL STOOD. are clear, and the opening THE CROSSING IS STILL. uses the same legible scale.

Priorities: Establish the crossing and its scale with a path and a tiny walker. Make the standing hill's footsteps and negative space legible. Give the dawn sky and ridge an environmental detail that carries across the reveal.

### 132 · Dayglass Relay

The right angles and pale beam read clearly against the sea-glass grid, though the checkerboard competes with the mirrors and the beam is visibly offset from its dark backing. The geometric puzzle plays to NAPLPS's strengths. An optics puzzle is familiar, and the sleeping lens is its distinctive hook, but the lens already looks powered and the selected mirror has no role in the current route. DAYGLASS RELAY, A TURN MIRROR and MOVES 07 read cleanly.

Priorities: Show a failed route that the selected mirror can actually fix. Distinguish the intended route and sleeping lens from the live beam. Align the sockets and give the board a quieter substrate.

### 133 · Drum of Moths

The lane hues and cream timing line are clear even when several cues are close together. Native mosaics make sense for a timing game, but the U shapes resemble brackets and the timing band reads as a solid plank. The combination of moths and percussion is unusual, but little of that comes through visually. DRUM OF MOTHS and A, S, D, F, G are all readable.

Priorities: Build moth silhouettes around the mosaic wings. Make the strike zone read as five playable pads and emphasize the active one. Add performance feedback in the unused header space.

### 134 · Terrace Kin

The warm field and cool beds separate well, and depth-ordered polygons produce convincing block heights. The oversized flat slab and identical triangle trees make the settlement feel like a tile test. The game action is clear from the outlined target and HUD, but the map has little identity beyond its terraces. TERRACE KIN, A PLANT and SEED 06 read clearly.

Priorities: Connect the beds to a visible water source. Give planted rows and the unplanted target recognizable agricultural detail. Model the foundation as land with a readable front edge.

### 135 · Keel of Thunder

The broad mirrored wings attract the eye and the citron edges and orange nodes pop against the near-black field. Symmetric facets and synchronized palette animation suit this machine, but the void behind it looks empty and the yellow zigzags end in midair. The boss reads like a familiar winged shooter silhouette and the grounding objective gives it a more specific identity. KEEL OF THUNDER and GROUND BOTH NODES are readable. The depleted part of the health bar merges into a single strip.

Priorities: Give the arcs visible grounding targets and make the player ship purposeful in that arena. Add storm depth and functional detail to the wing surfaces. Keep all nine health segments visible.

## Changes made

- **126 · Hem of the Sky** — Cut a jagged seam into the sky and stitched it toward the kite. Separated the kite's lit face from the rest, added spars and a hub, and adjusted the start lettering inside its button.
- **127 · Spore Switch** — Added bark grain, stem roots and darker gill bands. Faceted the beetle's shell and made unlit HUD markers hollow with a brighter rim.
- **128 · Siltfold** — Added recession contours, shallow shelves and water marks. Tied the ford sign to its waypoint and identified the two endpoints with weir and lift symbols.
- **129 · Tessera Vault** — Added rising arrows, a recessed field ring and a lit core in the cavity. Rebuilt all three property icons and moved the return control inward and the display caption below the ring.
- **130 · Brine Dash** — Replaced the slashes with sail-sled silhouettes and wakes. Inset the record tab and displayed the 0.4-second lead over second place.
- **131 · Hillwalk Oath** — Added a path, a staff-carrying walker and dawn clouds. Drew a stepping silhouette with a ground shadow, clearer feet and tiered fir trees in the reveal.
- **132 · Dayglass Relay** — Quieted the board, centered the beam in its channel and moved the selection to the mirror reflecting light upward. A dashed channel shows where the beam can go after a turn, and the lens reads as dormant.
- **133 · Drum of Moths** — Built bodies, antennae and wing spots around the mosaic cues. Broke the timing band into strike pads, outlined the active pad in gold and added COMBO 12.
- **134 · Terrace Kin** — Built a raised cistern with a spillway, added water to the furrows and replaced the rigid trees with flowering crops. Added planting pockets and gave the foundation an exposed clay edge clear of the HUD.
- **135 · Keel of Thunder** — Landed the arcs in grounding sockets, added storm layers and wing vents, redrew the player ship and separated the four depleted health segments.
