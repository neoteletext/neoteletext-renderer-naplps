# Set 7 · Improvement round 4

## Critique and priorities

I examined the pre-edit 480 × 460 and 640 × 480 renders. The lettering notes refer to the 480 × 460 versions.

### 126 · Hem of the Sky

The cream headline and coral sail balance each other across a deep blue sky. Native disc banding and overlapping polygon planes suit a title screen. Sewing torn weather from a kite is a distinctive premise, but the zigzag looks like a flying line and the bare lower facet looks unfinished. HEM OF, THE SKY, MEND THE WEATHER and PRESS START are clear. **Priorities** — show thread crossing both lips of the tear and show how the kite carries the thread.

### 127 · Spore Switch

The ascending coral caps make a clear platform route and the green player separates well from plum bark. The repeated relative lamp macro and arc fit the medium. Lighting a log by jumping is a good hook, but the loose background lines could belong to any cave and the last cap feels like another foothold. SPORE SWITCH, LAMPS and CROWN LOG are clear, including the solid and hollow counters. **Priorities** — give the log a cut end and build a recognizable destination beyond the last jump.

### 128 · Siltfold

The basin fills the sheet convincingly, with dark ink-blue pools against parchment. The defined hatch and nested polygon shorelines make a credible chart, and the temporary footpath is distinctive. The dashed route holds up, but the ford looks like a decorative strip and the endpoints use mismatched callouts. SILTFOLD, LOW WATER, WEIR, FORD, LIFT and WALK TO THE LIFT are readable. **Priorities** — show what the ford crosses and bring the location labels into the same visual hierarchy.

### 129 · Tessera Vault

The relic balances the vertical menu, and amber and mint separate cleanly on violet. Faceted fills and native rings work well for the imagined inventory. Trading physical properties is the original idea, but the caption gives no actionable effect and the identical angular trails look like display brackets. TESSERA VAULT, WEIGHT, ECHO, HEAT, 01, 02, 03, FALL UPWARD, A EQUIP and B RETURN are readable, with angular slashed zeroes. **Priorities** — show an effect on surrounding debris and make the selected property's consequence explicit.

### 130 · Brine Dash

The steep lanes and chalk, cobalt and orange hierarchy read immediately as race results. The polygons and fixed-pitch times are well used. The sail-sleds distinguish the fictional sport, although the small unlabelled gauges look like speed ornaments. BRINE DASH, SALT FLAT FINAL, ranks 1–4, PELL, VOSK, EMRU, NALV, 0:41.8, 0:42.2, 0:43.6, 0:45.1, NEW RECORD and 0.4 S LEAD are readable. **Priorities** — make the gaps independently readable and give the salt course a stronger identity in the header.

### 131 · Hillwalk Oath

The dawn disc frames the dark landform beautifully, but the pose reads like a low crawling animal and leaves little air below the belly. WAIT and palette redefinition make the living crossing a distinctive cutscene. HILLWALK OATH and THEN THE HILL STOOD. read clearly. **Priorities** — lift the body visibly across the cut and make the walker react to the movement.

### 132 · Dayglass Relay

The green grid is clean and the yellow beam is easy to trace. Native discs and wide strokes suit this invented optics puzzle. The off-board beam looks trapped in a plug, and the near-identical tile surfaces leave the input and goal underexplained. DAYGLASS RELAY, A TURN MIRROR and MOVES 07 are readable. **Priorities** — show where the lost light goes and visually connect the active control to the selected mirror and sleeping lens.

### 133 · Drum of Moths

Five colours and a clear vertical grid make a usable rhythm screen. The two native mosaics give the original insect cues real teletext character. The struck moth overlaps the drum rim and the timing boundary is hard to distinguish from the lane dividers. DRUM OF MOTHS, COMBO 12, A, S, D, F and G are clear. **Priorities** — give the whole field a timing line and show hit feedback independently of the cue.

### 134 · Terrace Kin

The centered axonometric garden has convincing depth and vivid clay and turquoise contrast. Polygon occlusion and contour strokes suit an invented builder. The selected plot reads as a crosshair over pebbles, and the water reaches the perimeter with little connection to the crops. TERRACE KIN, A PLANT and SEED 06 are clear. **Priorities** — show how the selected bed is fed and make the prospective planting recognizable.

### 135 · Keel of Thunder

The chartreuse keel and orange nodes break up a strong dark silhouette. Mirrored facets, rings and palette blink fit a terminal boss screen. The winged robot form is familiar, and the two grounded strikes look identical with little evidence of how the sockets work. KEEL OF THUNDER and GROUND BOTH NODES read clearly. **Priorities** — turn the sockets into visible discharge collectors and show the player sending the charge somewhere.

The opening Hillwalk Oath frame was also checked at 480 × 460. HILLWALK OATH and THE CROSSING IS STILL. are clear. The undisturbed trail reads as a continuous crossing, which makes it a useful reference for the reveal.

## Changes made

- **126 · Hem of the Sky** — Laced cream and coral thread across the tear, then traced the live thread from the sail’s spool to the needle.
- **127 · Spore Switch** — Cut a barred exit into exposed end grain and connected its lock to the last spore lamp.
- **128 · Siltfold** — Ran a stream between two pools and bridged it at the ford. Matched the three landmark plaques with vermilion rules and leaders.
- **129 · Tessera Vault** — Replaced the field brackets with rising stone chips and labelled the selected effect REVERSE GRAVITY.
- **130 · Brine Dash** — Replaced the unlabeled gauges with signed gaps in seconds and added shallow pans to the course header.
- **131 · Hillwalk Oath** — Repainted the revealed scene with the hill raised above the crossing, longer visible legs and the walker recoiling with their staff.
- **132 · Dayglass Relay** — Drew the beam escaping above the board, matched the turn key to the selected mirror and marked the prospective route with arrows.
- **133 · Drum of Moths** — Added a shared timing line, moved the struck moth onto it and drew a separate pulsing ripple on the active drum.
- **134 · Terrace Kin** — Branched the spillway into the selected terrace with a sluice and drew a pale preview of the crop row.
- **135 · Keel of Thunder** — Added collector prongs, lateral links from the player ship and cables carrying the charge away along the arena floor.
