# Set 7 · Improvement round 3

## Before the edits

Each pair was examined at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480. The taller proportions of the smaller preview make circles and short glyphs narrower. Text observations below refer to 480 × 460.

### 126 · Hem of the Sky

The oversized cream title and coral kite divide the blue field well. Disc banding and faceted fills give it a convincing period title-screen silhouette. Weather repair by kite and thread is an original premise, but the thin zigzag reads as a tether and the lower hills as flat filler. HEM OF, THE SKY, MEND THE WEATHER and PRESS START are all readable. **Priorities** — make the gap ahead of the stitches look torn, and turn the lower landscape into overlapping woven ground.

### 127 · Spore Switch

The ascending pink caps and acid-green jumper make a clear platform-game composition. Relative macro lamps, stepped fills and the arc suit the terminal. Fungal lamp-lighting is distinctive, but the identical caps and angular background sticks make the log feel like a generic cave. SPORE SWITCH, LAMPS and CROWN LOG are readable, and the two solid and two hollow counters are distinguishable. **Priorities** — build a recognizable broken bark ceiling, and differentiate lit platforms from the two landing objectives.

### 128 · Siltfold

Indigo residual pools and the cream-backed route contrast well on parchment. The programmed silt hatch and nested outlines feel like a chart and make the drained-lake idea original. The broad dashed path is easy to follow, but the small route endpoints say little about walking across an exposed bed, and the water marks look pasted on. SILTFOLD, LOW WATER, WEIR, FORD, LIFT and WALK TO THE LIFT are all readable. **Priorities** — add shoreline evidence of the previous water level, and make the ford a built crossing.

### 129 · Tessera Vault

The large amber relic balances the lilac inventory stack. NAPLPS planes and rings give the object convincing facets, but the display reads as a trophy case and the scattered crosses do little to sell the original gravity-trading idea. TESSERA VAULT, WEIGHT, ECHO, HEAT, 01, 02, 03, FALL UPWARD, A EQUIP and B RETURN are readable. The narrow slashed zero in each count is rough at this size. **Priorities** — visualize gravity locally in the display, and distinguish action keys from captions while enlarging the counts.

### 130 · Brine Dash

Cobalt, orange and chalk put the winner first, and the slanted polygon rows make an effective results screen. Sail-sled racing is original, though the vehicle stamps could be boats and the repeated speed lines make the middle column generic. BRINE DASH, SALT FLAT FINAL, ranks 1–4, PELL, VOSK, EMRU, NALV, 0:41.8, 0:42.2, 0:43.6, 0:45.1, NEW RECORD and 0.4 S LEAD are readable. The fixed-pitch decimal point is small. **Priorities** — draw visible runners, and make the row graphics convey time gaps.

### 131 · Hillwalk Oath

Coral against violet produces an effective dawn silhouette and the tiny traveler gives it scale. The palette change and staged polygons make an original living-landscape cutscene. The central dark mass is empty enough to resemble a turtle, and the legs blend into the low foreground. HILLWALK OATH and THEN THE HILL STOOD. are readable in the final image. The opening caption, THE CROSSING IS STILL., is also readable at this size. **Priorities** — make the body recognizably geological, and add separation around the moving legs and broken crossing.

### 132 · Dayglass Relay

The light beam and ivory mirrors stand clearly on sea glass. Rectangles and native rings make a credible puzzle board, but empty tile dots everywhere compete with the dashed route and the top arrow could be mistaken for the goal. Redirecting sunlight into an iris is the distinctive part. DAYGLASS RELAY, A TURN MIRROR and MOVES 07 are readable. **Priorities** — make the escaping beam end at a visible exhaust, and give the dormant channel and iris a common visual system.

### 133 · Drum of Moths

The five saturated lanes are orderly against charcoal and the cream title stays clear. Native mosaics and palette blink are a good match for this original insect-percussion idea. Long rows of flat rectangles look like a debug grid and the struck moth nearly merges into its matching pad. DRUM OF MOTHS, COMBO 12 and the keys A, S, D, F and G are readable, though COMBO has the thinnest strokes. **Priorities** — make the bottom row read as real drums, and reserve a distinct hit treatment for the active lane.

### 134 · Terrace Kin

Apricot, clay and turquoise make a strong readable isometric diorama. Polygon faces and occlusion do the work efficiently, and a water-fed mountain garden suits a builder. The repeated beds suggest a demonstration board, the bare rows look like sticks and the spillway fades into the platform edges. TERRACE KIN, A PLANT and SEED 06 are readable. **Priorities** — give the irrigation system a legible source and connection, and make the beds show different stages of cultivation.

### 135 · Keel of Thunder

Dark steel and citron keep the symmetric boss imposing while the orange nodes remain immediately visible. Mirrored facets and zigzag strikes fit the medium, but the winged silhouette is familiar and the grounding sockets look like small props rather than interactive targets. KEEL OF THUNDER and GROUND BOTH NODES are readable. The segmented gauge has no text. **Priorities** — make each socket a target on the arena floor, and give the wing surfaces recognizable electrical machinery.

## Changes made

- **126 · Hem of the Sky** — Added a steel sail needle and the frayed gap ahead of it, then worked woven folds into the foreground.
- **127 · Spore Switch** — Built a split bark canopy and distinguished the glowing, spotted caps from the marked landing zones.
- **128 · Siltfold** — Traced the old waterline around the exposed bed, drew a masonry ford beneath its marker and staggered the pool ripples.
- **129 · Tessera Vault** — Wrapped the relic in rising particle tracks, enlarged the inventory counts and boxed the action keys.
- **130 · Brine Dash** — Gave all four sleds raised runners and replaced the wakes with markers scaled to each racer's time behind the leader.
- **131 · Hillwalk Oath** — Drew rock strata across the hill, exposed the broken edge of the crossing and picked out the moving legs.
- **132 · Dayglass Relay** — Added a beam exhaust vent, cleared the empty tile centers and connected the dark channel directly to the sleeping iris.
- **133 · Drum of Moths** — Built drum shells with lugs beneath the heads, gave the landed moth a two-colour impact flash and enlarged the combo counter.
- **134 · Terrace Kin** — Built a working-looking fountain and marked the spillway junction, then planted intermediate growth in two beds.
- **135 · Keel of Thunder** — Cut cooling fins into both wings and marked the grounding sockets with concentric arena targets.
