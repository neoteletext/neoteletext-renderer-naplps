# Round 2 · Game screens 126–135

## Critique and priorities

Reviewed each incoming render at 480 × 460 and 640 × 480, including the opening WAIT tableau on 131. The narrower render compresses the horizontal proportions, making small symbols and close spacing more consequential.

### 126 · Hem of the Sky

The cream title and red kite balance each other, and the blue ridges give the palette depth. The disc, angular fills and pel lines are strong NAPLPS choices, but the sun's stripes look arbitrarily clipped and the scar stops almost dead at the kite. Weather repair is an original title-screen premise. The kite and pendant string could belong to an ordinary kite game. HEM OF, THE SKY, MEND THE WEATHER and PRESS START all read at 480. The start text sits too high in its cream box.

- Make the kite actively draw together two edges of a tear and make the thread visibly pass through them.
- Build the sun from deliberately clipped engraved bands and center the start label.

### 127 · Spore Switch

The ascending caps and lime beetle give the eye an obvious route through the plum field. Reusable lamp macros and the native arc suit a platformer, but the arc ends in the air and the unlit lamps look like pink arrowheads. Fungal jumping is familiar. A beetle lighting living lanterns gives this invented game a more specific interaction. SPORE SWITCH, LAMPS and CROWN LOG are readable at 480.

- Shape the lamps as open seed lanterns with unmistakable on and off interiors.
- Extend the jump trajectory from one cap to the next and give the log an organic interior with layered bark.

### 128 · Siltfold

Parchment and blue make a pleasing, well-contrasted chart. Native hatch and line textures suit a map, but the blue hatch looks like continuous water, making the pale path look like a bridge. The changing lake level is an original navigation premise and needs to read directly in the terrain. SILTFOLD, LOW WATER, WEIR, FORD, LIFT and WALK TO THE LIFT are legible at 480. The ford tag is conspicuous, while the endpoints are tiny and easy to miss.

- Make exposed silt and deep water visually different, with concentric shoreline shelves around the pools.
- Connect labels to landmarks consistently and redraw both endpoint structures.

### 129 · Tessera Vault

The amber selection and violet panels organize the screen well. Solid facets and native rings make clean icons, but the main relic looks like a stationary box with a floating lozenge painted on its lid. The inventory convention fits the theme, and equipping a physical property is original. TESSERA VAULT, FALL UPWARD, WEIGHT 01, ECHO 02, HEAT 03, A EQUIP and B RETURN read at 480, though the narrow slot labels are the least robust.

- Show gravity actually reversing with an open relic, separated floating core and a field lifting loose fragments.
- Make the selected slot project into the preview and give the preview caption its own space.

### 130 · Brine Dash

The cobalt header, orange winner and repeated slants give the score screen an effective hierarchy. Native filled polygons make sharp lanes, but four identical sleds waste the most illustrative column. The results screen fits immediately, and racing wind sleds on salt provides a distinctive setting. BRINE DASH, SALT FLAT FINAL, ranks 1–4, PELL, VOSK, EMRU, NALV, 0:41.8, 0:42.2, 0:43.6, 0:45.1, NEW RECORD and 0.4 S LEAD all read at 480.

- Give each sled a recognizable rig, with a stronger winner silhouette and wakes that stay distinct.
- Add a finish-line motif to the banner and echo it in the record tab.

### 131 · Hillwalk Oath

The dusk-to-coral palette shift and huge sun sell the scale. The WAIT reveal uses NAPLPS well and the walking landform is an original cutscene payoff. The opening hill looks barren because the trees pop in when it wakes. The solid rectangular foreground and joined legs spoil an otherwise convincing silhouette. HILLWALK OATH, THE CROSSING IS STILL. and THEN THE HILL STOOD. are all readable at 480.

- Keep the trees and rock strata continuous across the reveal, and cut the foreground into a natural ledge.
- Separate the stepping legs and use the broken path and disturbed ground to show what the movement does.

### 132 · Dayglass Relay

The bright beam is clear, but the heavy grid and empty tiles dominate the scene. Native discs and axis-aligned pels suit an optical game. The puzzle is familiar, and the escaping light and dormant lens are the page's particular hook. The preview currently runs through the selected mirror from top to bottom visually, and the exit arrow looks detached. DAYGLASS RELAY, A TURN MIRROR and MOVES 07 read at 480.

- Build a visible source, a connected escape port and a lens with a distinct iris.
- Integrate the real and possible routes into a quieter, beveled board with a stronger selected mirror.

### 133 · Drum of Moths

The five lane colours remain distinct on charcoal and the key labels are easy to scan. NAPLPS mosaics make appropriately chunky notes, but every wing outline is identical and there is little visual difference between beats and the screen background. Moth percussion is original as a premise. The current playfield could be rethemed by swapping one sprite. DRUM OF MOTHS, COMBO 12 and A, S, D, F, G are readable at 480.

- Give the moths alternating wing poses with markings that survive the small render.
- Tie them to percussion with drum-shaped strike targets and a pulse around the landed note.

### 134 · Terrace Kin

Apricot, teal and clay make the isometric model inviting. Depth-sorted solid polygons are a good NAPLPS fit, but the tilled plots look like grilles and the cistern drains into a wall. The planting interaction is clear. A shared-water hillside would distinguish this settlement from a generic plot builder. TERRACE KIN, A PLANT and SEED 06 read clearly at 480.

- Carry water from cistern to a visible lower channel along the foundation.
- Model furrows as earth and vary crops by growth stage. Add retaining courses to make the blocks read as terraces.

### 135 · Keel of Thunder

The wings have a strong silhouette, but their broad flat faces and open backdrop leave the encounter looking like a schematic. Native symmetric facets and shared palette BLINK suit an electrical boss. The mirrored ship is familiar, while diverting current into arena hardware is a distinctive objective. KEEL OF THUNDER and GROUND BOTH NODES both read at 480.

- Give the charge nodes mechanical housings and draw branched current into substantial grounding pylons.
- Frame the duel in a storm arena and make the central keel and player distinct from the two discharge paths.

## Changes made

- **126 · Hem of the Sky** — Drew two torn edges with paired eyelets and ran continuous gold thread through them to the needle. Clipped the sun's graduated bands to its silhouette and centered PRESS START in its panel.
- **127 · Spore Switch** — Added curved growth rings to the log, rebuilt the reusable lamp as a seed lantern with a bright or hollow core, and led the beetle's jump down to the next cap with a directional tip.
- **128 · Siltfold** — Changed the exposed bed to ochre silt and gave the deep pools nested blue shelves. Redrew the weir and lift and tied all three landmarks to the route with labelled callouts.
- **129 · Tessera Vault** — Lifted a faceted core and loose fragments above the open relic. Added a pointer from the active slot, strengthened all three slot labels and set FALL UPWARD in its own caption band.
- **130 · Brine Dash** — Gave the four sleds distinct rigs and staggered wakes, including a two-sail winner. Added slanted finish checks to the title and widened the record tab to accommodate its own checks clear of the lettering.
- **131 · Hillwalk Oath** — Established the trees and ridge strata before the WAIT. Cut a layered foreground ledge, then opened the crossing into a gap as the hill's separated feet step out and scatter chips of rock.
- **132 · Dayglass Relay** — Beveled the tiles and added socket centres, turned the beam endpoints into a source and escape port, and gave the sleeping lens a mechanical iris.
- **133 · Drum of Moths** — Added raised and level mosaic-wing poses with distinct eyespots. Made the strike targets into drumheads and framed the landed moth with impact marks in both BLINK states.
- **134 · Terrace Kin** — Joined the cistern to a foundation channel with a continuous stepped spillway, drew retaining courses, and separated freshly tilled earth, young shoots and flowering crops.
- **135 · Keel of Thunder** — Recessed the charge nodes in armored housings, branched the discharges into larger grounded fixtures, and established a perspective arena floor beneath the keel and player.
