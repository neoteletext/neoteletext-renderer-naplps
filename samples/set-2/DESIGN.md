# Twenty-five independent NAPLPS pages / 036–060

[Contact sheet](out/contact-sheet.png) · [Native small-screen sheet](out/contact-sheet-small.png) · [Manifest](out/manifest.json) · [Validation](out/validation.json) · [One-line summaries](out/SUMMARY.txt)

Each page has its own generator in `pages/`, its own sixteen-entry palette and a complete `.nap` still in `out/`. Same-stem PNGs are 640 × 480; suffixes `-256x200`, `-320x200` and `-512x400` identify the other required sizes. `out/review/` pairs each full-size image with its native 256 × 200 image. All twenty-five were visually inspected in both sizes, revised where needed and inspected again. No shared collection branding, page footer or fixed margin system is imposed. Names, services, books, prices and population figures are fictional; scientific symbols, countries, clock offsets and calendar dates use their ordinary meanings.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 render.py` to generate all streams, render the four sizes and assemble both sheets. Individual generators, such as `python3 pages/060-hearth-quilt.py`, also work directly. Run `python3 finish.py` for the manifest, design notes and welcome reveal; run `python3 validate.py` for the structural, determinism and predecessor checks. The encoder and page generators use Python’s standard library only. Preview assembly uses the already installed Pillow package. The renderer and Microstar font are read from the locations in BRIEF.md. Every generated or temporary file stays inside this directory.

## Validation

`validate.py` passed for all 25 deterministic generators and 100 final-size previews. It verified 7-bit encoding, the initial RESET, all sixteen initial palette entries, byte budgets, preview dimensions, visible-colour counts and the finite reveal. Every page was then rendered after every member of the set at both 640 × 480 and 256 × 200: all 1,250 comparisons matched standalone RGB pixels exactly, with empty renderer stderr. The detailed hashes and results are in `out/validation.json`.

## Encoding and renderer observations

Each stream explicitly designates primary G0, PDI G1 and mosaic G3 before entering SO and `20 7F 7F` RESET; it then designates macros in G2. This restores the sets as well as clearing inherited graphic state. DOMAIN uses four-byte coordinates, one-byte single values and an explicit logical pel. Sixteen SELECT COLOR / SET COLOR pairs define the complete palette before the background is painted. Important body fields are generally at least 1/32 of the screen wide; a few secondary dimension/timezone fields are slightly narrower and were checked at 256 × 200. Stroke text is exclusively Microstar, with no word-wrap controls, displayed cursors, input fields or transmit macros. All streams are 7-bit and under 6 KB; every page is also under 3 KB.

Required native features are distributed by purpose: G2 relative macros in 060, G3 DRCS in 050, downloaded fill pattern A in 041, hatching in 045 and 057, G3 mosaics in 048, incremental lines and proportional text in 040, supplementary accents/symbols in 042 and 057, colour mode 2 in 047, and final palette redefinition in 042 and 049. Only 049 is animated: one finite `3D 5C 48` WAIT, eight tenths of a second. No page uses BLINK. Every full-stream render is the complete intended final picture.

No new renderer defect was identified. Known documented limits were designed around: line textures need a real DOMAIN pel; arcs are limited to three points, with polygons used for the fox and heart; large display text can differ in thickness/placement from PP3; and the CLI draws through WAIT rather than stopping there. The welcome prefix is therefore cut at the exact recorded WAIT boundary for its reveal PNG and GIF. These previews use the supplied renderer, not PP3 oracle screenshots, and make no claim of pixel-identical period display typography. There are no speculative renderer workarounds or changes outside this directory.

## Pages

**036 — Cedar / Flat 2B** · [977 bytes](out/036-cedar-flat.nap) · [Preview](out/036-cedar-flat.png). Prussian blue and chalk-white walls give this small flat the feel of a working architectural drawing. Cyan windows, inward dimension arrows, opening arcs and restrained furniture symbols distinguish construction from annotation. The 8.4 by 5.5 metre envelope agrees with the 46.2 square metre area; the living-room label was moved clear of the entry swing.

**037 — September 1988** · [1,174 bytes](out/037-september.nap) · [Preview](out/037-september.png). A full-width red month tab, two binding rings and an ivory calendar grid make this a domestic desk calendar. September 1988 starts on Thursday; rent, the fair and a riverside walk have marked dates and a short key. The initial circular marks were replaced by roomy red rectangles so both digits remain white and readable at 256 pixels.

**038 — The Same Moment** · [1,293 bytes](out/038-same-moment.nap) · [Preview](out/038-same-moment.png). Four equal dials sit on warm grey with one vermilion minute hand per clock. London 06:00, Delhi 11:30, Tokyo 15:00 and Perth 14:00 show the same standard-time moment on 13 November. City names, digital readings and UTC offsets make the clocks usable; the grey annotation ink was darkened after low-resolution review.

**039 — Lantern Waltz** · [999 bytes](out/039-lantern-waltz.nap) · [Preview](out/039-lantern-waltz.png). An original two-bar flute miniature uses a polygon-built treble clef, five staff rules, slanted noteheads, stems, a beam and a phrase slur. The wide parchment field and quiet brown performance marks suggest engraved music rather than a computer dashboard. Each 3/4 bar contains three beats; the second has two quarter notes and two beamed eighths.

**040 — Fox and the Moon** · [947 bytes](out/040-fox-moon.nap) · [Preview](out/040-fox-moon.png). A sleepy curled fox rests between two broad sage hills under a cut-out crescent. Ear shapes, closed eyes and a cream tail brush carry the character without fine detail. Native INCREMENTAL LINE grasses use short two-bit moves; two large, proportionally spaced story lines close the scene in a dark inset at the bottom.

**041 — Green Fingers** · [1,449 bytes](out/041-green-fingers.nap) · [Preview](out/041-green-fingers.png). A tin gardener tries commanding a pot plant before discovering it needs sunlight. Three heavy-bordered panels use economical dialogue and a consistent repeated character; the last balloon points to the plant. The dotted background is a real downloaded texture A, defined with DEF TEXTURE, not a field of individually placed dots.

**042 — Birthday Magic** · [835 bytes](out/042-birthday-magic.nap) · [Preview](out/042-birthday-magic.png). A blush card for the fictional recipient Renée balances asymmetric balloon clusters around staggered BIRTHDAY letters. Loose strings and isolated confetti leave plenty of breathing room. The acute accent uses SS2 in the supplementary set; the ribbon colour is deliberately redefined after drawing, with no timed animation.

**043 — Pip's Market** · [874 bytes](out/043-pips-market.nap) · [Preview](out/043-pips-market.png). Three cream price cards advertise eggs, apples and milk with explicit pack or volume units. Red prices dominate yellow starbursts beneath the oversized WEEKLY WOW masthead. A diagonal corner cut supplies movement while the date band remains a solid red field; that revision stopped the first date digit disappearing into a yellow corner.

**044 — Leave by Stairs** · [1,102 bytes](out/044-leave-by-stairs.nap) · [Preview](out/044-leave-by-stairs.png). Black chevrons bracket a high-contrast evacuation instruction and a simple floor schematic. A red YOU point feeds a thick continuous green path through the clear lower corridor to OUT, with east-gate assembly directions beside it. The route was revised so it crosses the perimeter only at the marked doorway and never crosses an internal wall.

**045 — Paddock Prices** · [1,168 bytes](out/045-paddock-prices.nap) · [Preview](out/045-paddock-prices.png). A dark vertical landscape, rising sun and angular wheat sheaf give the page an agricultural identity. Horizontal native TEXTURE hatching suggests cut wood beneath the sheaf. Large, clearly denominated fictional saleyard prices occupy the ochre right column; the brown price ink was deepened for the smallest screen.

**046 — Marsh Library Card** · [601 bytes](out/046-library-card.nap) · [Preview](out/046-library-card.png). An offset paper shadow, punched hole, blue ruling and red margin turn the screen into a library index card. A fictional book, author, publisher and shelf location are set in large monospaced fields. The outlined ON SHELF stamp balances the otherwise deliberately plain bibliographic information.

**047 — Postroom** · [768 bytes](out/047-postroom.nap) · [Preview](out/047-postroom.png). Petrol navigation panels surround a pale four-message inbox with one highlighted unread item. Names, times and subjects have separate positions, so the hierarchy survives the small preview. The selected INBOX label and lower command bar use true colour-mode-2 character backgrounds; all prompts are display text, with no input-field or transmit commands.

**048 — Night Orchard** · [1,116 bytes](out/048-night-orchard.nap) · [Preview](out/048-night-orchard.png). Hot pink, cyan and phosphor green create a deliberately exuberant BBS rather than another sober panel interface. The border is made from actual G3 six-block mosaic characters, while four large numbered choices form the directory. Border rows were repositioned during review to keep both the NIGHT masthead and the choice prompt clear.

**049 — Merevale Link** · [438 bytes](out/049-merevale-link.nap) · [Preview](out/049-merevale-link.png). A block-built M and receding stripes identify the fictional regional Merevale network. Large blue lettering sits on cool grey, with a navy lower welcome band. One WAIT of eight tenths reveals PRESS # TO BEGIN and a final teal palette update; the complete stream always ends on the finished welcome screen. A GIF and a prefix still show the short reveal.

**050 — Find Your Way** · [967 bytes](out/050-keypad-guide.nap) · [Preview](out/050-keypad-guide.png). An oversized telephone keypad uses shallow shadows and ochre key marks, balanced by five concise command explanations. A home symbol is drawn once into a real DRCS character with DEF DRCS, then invoked beside 0 HOME. The keypad is illustrative: there are no cursor styles, form fields or input behaviour encoded.

**051 — Harbor Sales** · [751 bytes](out/051-harbor-sales.nap) · [Preview](out/051-harbor-sales.png). Four bars rise from a common zero baseline against subdued navy grid lines, with fronts, tops and sides in related shades. Quarterly net sales are 42, 58, 53 and 74 thousand dollars; the final quarter is singled out in gold. Values, quarter labels and the dollar-thousands legend are deliberately larger than conventional dashboard type.

**052 — Where It Goes** · [696 bytes](out/052-household-budget.nap) · [Preview](out/052-household-budget.png). A large pastel pie sits beside an open four-line legend for a fictional household of two. Native filled three-point arcs draw the curved sector boundaries and centre triangles complete each wedge. Housing, food, bills and saving sum to 100 percent and $2,400; fine paper-coloured seams separate the sectors.

**053 — River County Population** · [739 bytes](out/053-river-population.nap) · [Preview](out/053-river-population.png). A plum ground and two soft hues distinguish this compact demographic plate from the sales chart. Five age bands occupy a generous central gutter, with men extending left and women right on the same linear scale. Every bar contains its fictional population in thousands, avoiding a dense numerical axis.

**054 — The First 18** · [1,302 bytes](out/054-first-eighteen.nap) · [Preview](out/054-first-eighteen.png). Eighteen tiles retain the real main-group arrangement and group numbers 1, 2 and 13–18. Each contains a large element symbol and atomic number; the open space between hydrogen and helium preserves the periodic-table silhouette. Colours distinguish metals, noble gases and the remaining elements, so the legend does not misclassify boron or silicon as ordinary nonmetals.

**055 — The Solar System** · [832 bytes](out/055-solar-order.nap) · [Preview](out/055-solar-order.png). A cropped golden sun, sweeping concentric orbit arcs and eight ordered planets give this astronomy plate its horizontal momentum. Jupiter has broad belts and Saturn a native arc ring; staggered labels avoid a tiny numbered key. The caption explicitly says that only order is represented and sizes and gaps vary; this is not a physical distance-scale diagram.

**056 — The Human Heart** · [1,270 bytes](out/056-human-heart.nap) · [Preview](out/056-human-heart.png). A schematic four-chamber section uses blue for the anatomical right side and red for the left, with a pale septum and valve marks. Vena cava, aorta and all four chambers receive large labels; anatomical right appears on the viewer’s left. Chamber dots and elbowed leaders remain legible at low resolution, and the right-ventricle leader was moved clear of its word.

**057 — Water / H2O** · [602 bytes](out/057-water-molecule.nap) · [Preview](out/057-water-molecule.png). Three highlighted spheres and two broad bond sticks make a generous ball-and-stick water model. The oxygen sphere carries genuine cross-hatch shading; a native arc measures the 104.5-degree H–O–H angle. The degree sign comes from supplementary character 30 through SS2, and the chemical formula uses separately positioned normal stroke glyphs for its subscript.

**058 — Flags of Six Nations** · [577 bytes](out/058-six-flags.nap) · [Preview](out/058-six-flags.png). Japan, France, Sweden, the Bahamas, Vietnam and Czechia provide six recognisable flat flag constructions. The grid preserves each flag’s aspect ratio, including the Bahamas at 2:1 and Sweden at 16:10, rather than stretching them into identical boxes. Large country names replace fine explanatory captions; the Japanese disc, Nordic cross and triangle proportions are constructed geometrically.

**059 — Alder Arms** · [997 bytes](out/059-alder-arms.nap) · [Preview](out/059-alder-arms.png). Invented Alder arms place a gold chevron and three pale six-point mullets on an azure shield, framed by cut-cloth mantling. A grey barred helmet and twisted wreath establish the heraldic silhouette; a folded gold ribbon carries ROOTED IN COURAGE. The shield dominates a burgundy field without borrowing a real family’s arms.

**060 — Hearth Quilt** · [1,591 bytes](out/060-hearth-quilt.nap) · [Preview](out/060-hearth-quilt.png). Twenty pieced-star blocks fill a bordered textile in indigo, brick, sage and wheat. A relative star macro and a separate cross-stitch macro are translated and recoloured across the quilt; paper diamonds and fine seam rules supply the smaller scale. This page intentionally has no title or footer: the repeating fabric is the complete picture.
