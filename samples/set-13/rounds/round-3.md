# Round 3: critique of the round-2 renders

The critique below is of the renders in `rounds/round-2/`, looked at in both sizes. Every page now has its round-2 changes and reads correctly at 480 by 460; the faults left are of detail, light and use of the medium.

## 177 Good Morning, Hartley Avenue

The page works as a morning front page, with the menu marks and the lamp posts. The mail row still does not connect to anything on the page: the badge says "2 NEW" but no letters arrive. The sky has a cloud and birds; the sun has no halo, so its warmth stops at its outer ring. The date is missing from the title band, which is where a front page carries it.

Changes planned: a mailbox at the gate, with a red flag that blinks up and down, so MAIL has a picture; a faint halo ring round the sun in the pale colour; the date in the title band.

## 178 A Card for Grandma

The bouquet is clean, but the cream of the card is a flat field, and the bouquet floats in it with nothing behind it. A card of this period would have a printed paper texture. The medium has fill textures; the page uses none, and the dots that follow are drawn as discs, not as a texture.

Changes planned: a dot pattern of small blush discs over the cream, behind the greeting and the bouquet; two leaf sprigs behind the outer tulips, so the bouquet sits on foliage.

## 179 The Family Message Board

The board is complete and clear. The one gap is at the left, below DAD, which holds nothing. A board of this kind is made of pinned photographs as much as notes, and the medium's fill patterns could make a photograph without a picture file.

Changes planned: a small polaroid photograph pinned at the left under DAD, with a sky and a hill drawn in its frame.

## 180 Supper Tonight

The recipe page has its structure: the title, the dish, the timer, the two lists. Two things would help a reader use it: the ingredients have no tick boxes for a cook to tick, and the method does not show which step is current, which a timer page should.

Changes planned: a small square box before each ingredient; the current step, step 3, on a yellow band behind its line.

## 181 The Wired Living Room

The scene is now full: the lamp, the rug, the plant, the picture and the family. The sofa is almost hidden by the four people, so the room has no seat to sit on. The window is a flat sky, and light from it does not reach the room. Nothing on the wall says the time.

Changes planned: a bright patch of sunlight on the floor from the window, a pale yellow parallelogram; cushions on the sofa, as discs at its back; a wall clock between the window and the picture.

## 182 Neighbourhood Calendar, Maple Ridge Estates

The grid is now the most complete calendar in the set: bins, balloons, a garage sign, the coming-up panel. The grid lacks the weather a family would look at on a calendar, and the today box has no label, so a reader must find the red outline.

Changes planned: small weather marks on three days (a sun on 3, a cloud on 8, rain on 16); the word TODAY inside the today box, at its foot.

## 183 The House That Orders Its Own Paper Towels

The page is complete as a story: the stock, the flag, the terminal, the van and the order. The order form does not show that the order was placed, and the shelves hold no counts.

Changes planned: a green tick beside DELIVER THU 8 AM, drawn last with WAIT, so the order is confirmed on the page; a small count tag on each shelf row ("x6", "x4", "x12"), drawn in white on the shelf.

## 184 Home Watch

The plan is complete. The kitchen is lit only by its fill, and the status panel has no sign of the smoke detector being well beyond its text.

Changes planned: three small lamp discs on the kitchen ceiling, drawn in the porch lamp's entry so they blink with it; a green ring round the smoke detector, to show that it is working.

## 185 Tonight's Horoscope

The horoscope is clear and full, and it is the page the set's reader would look at longest. The wheel's hub is a flat disc with a word. The rings of the wheel could carry more structure.

Changes planned: two thin rings inside the segments, round the hub, in navy, so the wheel reads as an instrument.

## 186 School Bus Is Late

The bulletin reads, with the bus on the road, the snowbank below its wheels, the stop arm and the foot line. The page has no people, and a school-bus bulletin without children at the stop tells a parent less than it could.

Changes planned: three children at the roadside by the stop sign, drawn as heads and bodies in their coats, so the stop has someone waiting.

## Summary of the round

Two pages remain weakest: 178, whose card is a flat field, and 181, whose room has no seat and no light from the window. Both changes above give them texture and light. Shared across the set: no page uses the medium's own texture or pattern commands; the dot patterns of 178 and 179 are drawn as rows of discs. The round-3 changes add a disc pattern to 178 and a light patch to 181, without a texture command.
