# Round 4: critique of the round-3 renders

The critique below is of the renders in `rounds/round-3/`, looked at in both sizes. Round 3 added the detail named in `round-3.md`. Round 4 looks at what that detail did to each page, and at what is still weak.

## 177 Good Morning, Hartley Avenue

The mailbox, the halo, the date and the lamp posts all sit well. The sun's halo reads as a light, which is the aim. Remaining: the panel's four marks are as large as the labels and crowd them; the lamp posts are the only street furniture, and they are thin.

Changes planned: the menu marks are reduced by a fifth so the labels breathe; the lamp posts have a wider pole and a larger globe.

## 178 A Card for Grandma

The printed dot pattern works and gives the cream a paper feel. The leaf sprigs added in round 3 are too big and too angular: they read as arrowheads, and they cover the tulips' stems rather than sitting behind the flowers.

Changes planned: the sprigs become slim, curved leaves drawn as two-point shapes, shorter than the tulips, with their tips turned outward; they are set lower, under the cups.

## 179 The Family Message Board

The polaroid completes the board. Every note, pin and photograph is clear. The board now has no gap, and the page's finish is high. There is no change this round that would improve it materially; the page is left as it is.

## 180 Supper Tonight

The tick boxes and the current-step band work. The page is complete as a recipe card. A reader would still look first at the timer's number, and the 25 in the hub is set in the same red as the title. The bowl beside INGREDIENTS floats below the heading's line.

Changes planned: the hub's number is set in the navy of the timer's rings, to separate it from the red of the title; the bowl beside the heading moves up onto the heading's line.

## 181 The Wired Living Room

The light patch added in round 3 is drawn after the people, so it covers their legs and reads as a pale mat in front of the sofa, not as light. The rest of the room is complete. The figures' arms and the sofa are now well placed.

Changes planned: the light patch is drawn before the sofa and the people, so they stand in it; it becomes three thin pale stripes, as sunlight falls through blinds, rather than one slab.

## 182 Neighbourhood Calendar, Maple Ridge Estates

The calendar now carries weather, bins, balloons, a sign and a today label. The weather marks are small: the sun on the 3rd is a dot, and the cloud on the 8th is three grey discs with little presence. A calendar of this kind would show the weather a size that can be read.

Changes planned: the sun on the 3rd gets rays; the cloud on the 8th is drawn as a single cloud shape with a flat base; the rain on the 16th gets a fourth stroke.

## 183 The House That Orders Its Own Paper Towels

The page carries its story: the stock, the flag, the counts, the sun, the window, the van, the order and its tick. The count tags sit across the rolls on the second shelf and read a little cramped. The van is cut off by the left edge of the page.

Changes planned: the count tags move to the shelves' right ends, clear of the rolls; the van is moved in from the edge so its full body shows.

## 184 Home Watch

The plan carries light: the kitchen lamps, the study lamp, the porch lamp and the smoke detector's green ring. The kitchen lamps read as dark dots in the still because their blink is in its off state. The thermostat's needle is thin.

Changes planned: the kitchen lamps are given a glow ring so they read as lamps in the still, as the porch lamp does; the thermostat needle is thicker.

## 185 Tonight's Horoscope

The horoscope is complete: the wheel, its hub rings, the planets, the moon, the reading, the lucky colours and tomorrow's line. The stars in the dark are small dots; the picture lacks a rating, which was not added in round 2 for lack of room.

Changes planned: the stars are drawn as four-pointed sparkles, larger, in the planet ring's places; no rating is added.

## 186 School Bus Is Late

The bulletin reads, with the bus, the road, the snowbank, the stop arm and the foot line. The children at the stop are too small to read as people at the channel's size, and the stop arm is a small red disc.

Changes planned: the children are drawn at one and a half times their size; the stop arm is made an octagon of the stop sign's size, with a white bar across it.

## Summary of the round

Most pages are now complete. The changes this round are of finish: the light on 181, the leaves on 178, the weather marks on 182, the glow on the lamps on 184, and the scale of the children on 186. Shared across the set: the blink states show in the still as one of two colours; where a still matters, the page now sets a glow or a second mark so the feature is readable either way.
