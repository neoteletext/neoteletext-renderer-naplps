# Set 13: concepts

Ten pictures of videotex as its makers imagined it in 1979 to 1986, numbered 177 to 186. They are group A of the research in `~/.cache/codex-naplps/batch/videotex-research.md`, section 3 (home and family), in its order. Every service, brand, company, place and person in them is invented; "Telidon", "NAPLPS" and "videotex" are not drawn on any picture.

## Changes to the concepts

- **Renamed, to keep every name invented:** the research's "HomeLine" is NEIGHBOURLINE; "Sentinel Home" is WARDWELL HOME WATCH; "Starwheel" is ORRERY DAILY; "KitchenFax" stays, as an invented name.
- **Arc in place of a rotated italic (178):** NAPLPS rotates text in quarter turns only, so the greeting's letters are placed one at a time along an arc.
- **Added beyond the concept (177, 179, 183, 186):** the bus on the road, the polaroid, the delivery van and the bus's stop arm and children, each to carry a detail the concept's text names.
- **Concept 10, the snowflakes:** drawn as programmable characters, as the brief's medium list asks.
